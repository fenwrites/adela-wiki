*Kindly schoolteacher and mother to Illesara*

#npc

## Summary
[[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]'s mother (pronounced "Low-ay-tay"), the [[Glimmerglade]]'s schoolteacher, about 220 years old. She is short, with long frizzy hair going grey, and worries about her daughter. [[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]], originally of the [[Stonyglade]], moved to the Glimmerglade to be with her, as gnome custom expects.
