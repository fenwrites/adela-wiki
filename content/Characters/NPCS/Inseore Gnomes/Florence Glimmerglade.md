*Elderly matriarch of the glimmerglade and storyteller of gnomes*

#npc

[[Inseore Gnomes]]

## Summary
Florence is the leader of the [[Glimmerglade]]: a hardy, joking old lady who always has candy on her, and an expert in woodcraft. She is a cleric of Arshanta, 328 years old, and a cousin of [[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]] and [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]. She is looking for an apprentice.

## Activities and Rumours
- She told the party the story of the [[Shimmerwyld]]'s origin, and of the Inseore's shame.
- She told [[Mona]] about the Koro spirits (they love cheese).
- When the gnomes left the Shimmerwyld, she gave [[Klau]] a plain book of the stories she has told over the years.
