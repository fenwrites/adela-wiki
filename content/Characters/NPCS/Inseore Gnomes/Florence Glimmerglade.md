*Elderly matriarch of the glimmerglade and storyteller of gnomes*

#npc

[[Inseore Gnomes]]

## Appearance and Quirks
Wispy white hair, often with twigs caught in it; a big warm smile despite some rotten teeth; wrinkly gnome skin with a few hairs in odd places. She usually wears a simple dark green gown with a leather belt hung with small pouches of herbs for her sinuses, sore feet and other ailments.

## Summary
Florence is the leader of the [[Glimmerglade]]: a hardy, joking old lady who always has candy on her, and an expert in woodcraft. She is a cleric of Arshanta, 328 years old, and a cousin of [[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]] and [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]. She is looking for an apprentice.

## Activities and Rumours
- She told the party the story of the [[Shimmerwyld]]'s origin, and of the Inseore's shame.
- She told [[Mona]] about the Koro spirits (they love cheese).
- When the gnomes left the Shimmerwyld, she gave [[Klau]] a plain book of the stories she has told over the years.

- At the gnome camp on the edge of the Shimmerwyld, she told the children about the first time the [[Inseore Gnomes]] left the forest, over 300 years ago, to negotiate its protection with the newly formed [[Saers]] and [[Hjirolis]]. She was only a young girl then, but a boy she grew up with went with his family to the capitals, and became a great hero and protector of the Shimmerwyld.
- She is grateful for the party's help and trusts them, [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]] most of all.
