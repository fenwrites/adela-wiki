*Pragmatic leader of the Sunglade*


## Appearance and Quirks
Agatha is a middle-aged forest gnome with dark greenish-brown skin, shoulder-length red hair, and hazel eyes. She is average height for a dwarf, and is often wearing a simple sundress and sunhat, with a few pouches and whatnot tied to it.
## Summary
Agatha Sunglade is a **settlement leader** operating out of [[Sunglade]]. She is young for a glade warden, and pragmatic, often preferring action to the slow meandering of other gnomes. She is well-liked, though can be abrasive at times.
## Relationships
- [[Dotty Sunglade]]: Agatha's daughter, who is reluctantly being trained to be a future glade warden.
- [[Florence Glimmerglade]]: Agatha's longtime friend, Florence briefly mentored Agatha but complained she was too feisty.
## Activities and Rumours
- Agatha dabbles in a lot of hobbies, but isn't skilled at any task in particular. Some say this is why she became warden in the first place.
- Agatha agreed to [[The Party]]'s plan of leading the [[Glimmerglade]] and [[Sunglade]] gnomes temporarily out of the [[Shimmerwyld]] when faced with Infernal Events in the Shimmerwyld CE374. She sent her daughter Dotty and burgeoning warrior Sorven Sunglade along with them.
