*Serious but caring grove leader-in-training*




## Summary
Dotty Sunglade is a **guide and healer** operating out of [[Sunglade]].

## Relationships
- **[[Agatha Sunglade]]**: Mother
- **Sorven Sunglade**: Cousin
- **[[The Party]]**: Helped [[the party]] during their time in the [[Shimmerwyld]]

- Serious, with a buzz cut, and keen to become a medic. She tends everyone's cuts and rashes each evening, and collects leeches.
- She travelled with the party out of the [[Shimmerwyld]] to the [[Orreni Family|Orreni]] lands, where she works with the Orreni on the gnomes' behalf.
