*Eccentric inventory of the gnomes and father to Illesara*

#npc

## Summary
[[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]'s father, an artificer of about 300 years who researches the [[Shimmerwyld]]: its trees, plants, ground, rocks, water and magic. Originally from the [[Stonyglade]], he moved to the [[Glimmerglade]] to be with Loette, as gnome men traditionally do. Beardless and carefree, he calls his wife [[Loette Glimmerglade|Loette]] "Tay". The family lives about a thirty-minute walk from the [[Glimmerglade]].

## Activities and Rumours
- His tinkering shack is full of magic items, and a few have wandered into town. Inventions include FastGro Fertilizer, a wrist crossbow, a wooden spirit sensor that rattles when spirits are near ("bah, doesn't work, you want it?") and a tool that changes shape when you swear.
- He suggested the Glasspetal flower might help against the blight, and was given the horn of an infernal to study.
