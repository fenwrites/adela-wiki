*Illy's old teacher at the Lyceum, a gnome who left the Shimmerwyld behind*

## Summary
Bartleby Hannely is a gnome teacher at the Lyceum of Alcion, originally from the [[Shimmerwyld]]. He left that life behind, recruited [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] on a visit home, and trained her as his apprentice.

## Activities and Rumours
- Illy wrote ahead to say she was coming, and to ask whether he could help the party into the university. He hasn't replied yet.
