*Archon of Ceropolis and lover of indulgement and arts*



## Appearance and Quirks
[[Hjirolis#Political Structure|Archon]] [[Aoran Aurelius]] is a middle-aged human man with bronze skin. He is overweight, often oiled, and wears a toga adorned in golden trinkets that barely covers him.
## Summary
Aoran Aurelius is the Archon of [[Ceropolis]]. He is known to be a lover of the arts, and spends much of his time appreciating poetry, writing, painting, and the human form. His titles include Architect of Splendor, Keeper of the Marble Lyre, Golden Patron of [[Ceropolis]], and Master of the Radiant Forum.
## Relationships
- [[Othenios Vyrik]]: his aide and historian, and his lower-born cousin.
## Activities and Rumours
- [[Ceropolis]] was not always so prosperous, and Aoran has steadily benefitted from the increasing wealth that the regional agriculture has generated. Despite his reputation for opulence, he is also considered a competent leader that keeps up to date with politics.
- Aoran's official stance on the [[Hjir and Sjuvven Tensions]] is indifference. He claims to respect the opinions of the populace and whatever outcome they might seek. His opposition claim that he secretly employs Sjuvven to admire their bodies, but this is largely unsubstantiated.
- Rumours claim that Aoran is upset to yet be uninvited to a position in [[Ankarnassas]] by the [[Hjirolis#Political Structure|Autokrator]].

- He is uninterested in the [[Shimmerwyld]]. Ceropolis is expanding its farmland in that direction, but is in no rush to push the forest back.
