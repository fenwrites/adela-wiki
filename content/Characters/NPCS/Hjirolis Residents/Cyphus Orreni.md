*A wandering son of the Orreni family, searching for his missing cousin*

## Summary
Anax Cyphus Orreni, second son of the [[Orreni Family]], a noble of [[Hjirolis]] who chose adventure over the military or a household post. The party met him in the [[Shimmerwyld]] with his bugbear guard, [[Graveltooth]], searching for his young nephew Iocles, missing for two weeks. He and Graveltooth joined the gnome caravan, helped search the Witchwood, and escorted the party to his family's estate.

## Activities and Rumours
- He knows [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] "speaks to the forest".
- He brought the party the news that the [[Divine of Order]] had taken the name Apolus.
