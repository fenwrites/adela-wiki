*Cyphus Orreni's bugbear guard*

## Summary
Graveltooth is the bugbear guard of [[Cyphus Orreni]]. The two met the party in the [[Shimmerwyld]] while searching for Cyphus's missing nephew, Iocles, joined the gnome caravan, and later escorted the party to the Orreni estate.
