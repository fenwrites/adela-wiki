[[Ceropolis]]
[[Hjirolis]]
[[Fibbart Miller]]

## Appearance and Quirks
A human man of perhaps thirty, of average height, with a V-shaped, shiny bald head and sunken, slightly birdish eyes. He wears a full-length white robe with green accents.

## Summary
A local guide and historian who met the party at the port of [[Pelethra]] and led them into [[Ceropolis]].

## Activities and Rumours
- At the gate of Apolus he told them: *"We stand in the shade of the gate of Apolus, Divine of Order and God-Emperor of the once great nation of Isiliosi... Order is a shield against chaos. The strong must guide the weak. The law is eternal and immutable."*
