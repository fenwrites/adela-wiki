*The reed-thin head of the Growth chapter in Ankarnassas*

## Appearance and Quirks
An older man in a friar's cap, tall and thin as a reed.

## Summary
Head of the [[Divine of Growth|Growth]] chapter in [[Ankarnassas]], of the order Auxesis Fylakas. He is people-first, and less nature-oriented than [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] might be used to.

## Activities and Rumours
- His chapter works from an old stone-brick temple, caring for the poor and needy through a spreading flu. It's more rigid than other chapters.
- [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] told Delrek to seek him out, warning that the chapter's views are shaped by Order.
- He has spoken with Delrek briefly, and the chapter asked Delrek to collect herbs from a nearby cavern ([[Session 23 Recap|Session 23]]).
