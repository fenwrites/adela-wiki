*An imposing but kind Sjuvven tavern owner, and a friend of Illesara*

## Appearance and Quirks
Tall, muscled and bald, with tattoos. Imposing to look at, but actually quite kind. Goes by Twunk.

## Summary
A [[Sjuvven]] bar owner in Thema Pende, who runs [[The Tall Order|the Tall Order]], and a friend of [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]'s.

## Relationships
- [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]: friend.

- The Tall Order's cook and server, a single mother he sees almost as an adopted mother.

## Activities and Rumours
- Spent an evening trading news with [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] at the Tall Order, and told her what he knows of [[Big Red]] and the [[Redwoods]] ([[Session 24 Recap|Session 24]]).
