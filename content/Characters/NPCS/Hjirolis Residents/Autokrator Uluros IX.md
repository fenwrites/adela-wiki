*The current Autokrator of Hjirolis*

The current [[Hjirolis#Political Structure|Autokrator]] of [[Hjirolis]], ruling from [[Ankarnassas]]. He is about 60 years old, of the Isokrates family. His father calmed the chaos that followed the [[Sjuvven]] revolt, then died on his son's 20th birthday, and Uluros was crowned.

## As the Street Sees Him
- In Thema Tessra and above, nearly everyone respects him as a strong and decisive leader.
- In Thema Pende, opinion splits between people who respect the law and the [[Astynomoi]], and those who ignore all of that and just want to live their lives.
- Since a failed assassination attempt he hasn't left Elikos for some time, though he still regularly appears in court there.
