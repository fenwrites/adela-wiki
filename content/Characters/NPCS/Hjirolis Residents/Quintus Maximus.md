*A secretive envoy of Hjirolis*

#npc

## Appearance and Quirks
Short and square: hairy, broad and muscular, with short black hair, a goatee and a square jaw.

## Summary
A secretive [[Hjirolis|Hjir]] envoy, returning home aboard the [[HES Longsail]]. He keeps to himself, speaks with a thick Hjir accent, and says he is visiting family.
