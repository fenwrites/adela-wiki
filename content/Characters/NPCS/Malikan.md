*The great emerald dragon of dragonborn legend*

In the legend recorded in [[The Chronicle]], Malikan the Magnificent was an emerald dragon who, some 1,200 years ago, tested Cormac, the dragonborn who wished to unite his people. Dragonborn in [[Saers]] grow up knowing they share a link to this creature of legend.
