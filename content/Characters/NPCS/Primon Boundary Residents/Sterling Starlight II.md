## Appearance and Quirks
A silver dragonborn: serious, troubled and distant, with an air of anger and disappointment, especially toward [[Klau]].

## Summary
[[Klau]]'s father, and a tailor in [[Primon Boundary]] (his rival in the trade is [[Isadore]]). A single father, he married [[Anna Lee Hammersmak]], [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]]'s mother, who runs the Primon Inn, where he helps out a little. Their daughter Samira is half-sister to Klau and Delrek.

## Activities and Rumours
- When Samira fell sick with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]], he spoke with [[Audric Malikan|Audric]], who gave the family funds to take her to a Temple of Growth in [[Wardensport]].
