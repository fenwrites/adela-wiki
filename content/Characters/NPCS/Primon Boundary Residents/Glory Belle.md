*Ex-mercenary captain of guard in Primon Boundary and proud mother of Garnet*

[[Garnet Teimster]]
[[Primon Boundary]]

## Appearance and Quirks
Tomboyish, fond of drink and a good time, and a deadly fighter. As a parent she's loosey-goosey, more friend than mother.

## Summary
Primon Boundary's captain of the guard, and a former mercenary who fought all over the southern coast of [[Nosovae]], mostly out near the eastern [[The Seven Cities|Seven Cities]]. She retired to escape accusations of piracy (which were false), tired of fighting, and in love. She is married to [[Isadore]]; together they are [[Garnet Teimster|Garnet]]'s mothers.

## Activities and Rumours
- At the summit she estimated four months to raise a force of 200 to clear the Shimmerwyld's dangers.
