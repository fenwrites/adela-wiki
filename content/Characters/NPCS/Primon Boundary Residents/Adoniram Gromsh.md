*Missionary to Divine of Growth and general grump*


## Appearance and Quirks
Gromsh is a middle-aged dwarf with greying-brown shoulder-length hair, dark brown eyes, and a few scars on his face. He is usually wearing his [[Divine of Growth]] vestments, which consists of a single long deep green robe and white cloth belt. Gromsh has a heavy accent which makes him nearly unintelligible in Common, but speaks eloquently in dwarven.
## Summary
Adoniram Gromsh is a **priest of Growth** operating out of [[Primon Boundary]]. He is one of the few dwarves living in [[Saers]], having originally come from the dwarven homeland of [[Hasitoppe]].
## Relationships
- [[Delrek Hammersmak]]: Gromsh introduced him to Growth, helping train him physically and mentally.
## Activities and Rumours
- Gromsh spends most of his time within his small chapel to growth, meditating in nature, or blessing local crops. Other duties includes delivering new babies and acting as an official ambassador of Growth in eastern [[Saers]].
- Gromsh is not a fan of the status-quo in [[Saers]], and will usually be one of the few people to go against the grain of Community at times. He is, however, approving of the official sanctioned status of Growth, and begrudging acknowledges that Community and Growth go hand in hand at times.
- Gromsh likely came from [[Hasitoppe]] because Growth is not a very commonly worshipped Divine there, but he rarely speaks about his past.
