*Expert merchant brought in to deal with the Green Death frontier*

[[Saers]]

## Appearance and Quirks
Irreverent and friendly, but deadly serious when it comes to business.

## Summary
Primon Boundary's Trade Master. The Saers council brought her in from her post as trades expert to [[The Seven Cities]] to manage the town's economy on the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] frontier. At the summit, she warned that the town had about two years of grain in stock, and could afford perhaps four years of food without its agricultural exports.

## Relationships
- [[Tharr Brirr]]: her apprentice for a few weeks before the party left; enough that he can still call on her for information. She's busy, but interested in hearing what he finds.
