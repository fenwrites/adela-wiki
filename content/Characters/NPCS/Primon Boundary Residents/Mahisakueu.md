*Elderly, wise, and mystical matriarch of the Naltése*

#npc

## Summary
Lady Mahi, chief of the [[Naltése Peoples]] near [[Primon Boundary]]. At the summit she urged the town to take its time, pointing out they had at least thirty days before anyone would starve.

## Activities and Rumours
- She held an incense ceremony to bless Tharr's journey, and gave him three bundles of Naltése incense.
