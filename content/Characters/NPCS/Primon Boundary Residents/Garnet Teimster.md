## Appearance and Quirks
A red tiefling who carries herself with confidence, though she's still learning how to be "cultured" in the city. Her frankness gets her treated as a charming curiosity ("look at this darling tiefling") more than true charm. She is fiercely friendly and loving toward the party, whom she cares for like family. Mercantilism interests her, but adventure interests her more.

## Summary
A tiefling from [[Primon Boundary]] with a bad leg from a childhood break, and a good friend of the party, who helped her escape her small town. She sailed with them to [[Hjirolis]] (finding her sea legs quickly) and stayed in [[Ceropolis]] to learn the merchant trade with Tharr's backing.

## Activities and Rumours
- She invested in a carpenter supplying carts and made back her losses. "Klau's Quills" is in business.
- She sewed each of the Primon three a patch: a three-claw signature for Klau, a tree for Delrek and a scale for Tharr.

- She is a childhood friend of the Primon three. Her leg was broken on their fateful trip into the [[Shimmerwyld]] during the Choosing festival, and the bond formed there lasted: she wrote letters to Klau and Tharr, and spent a lot of time with Delrek in Primon.
- "Klau's Quills" is Tharr's push to make the party a proper company, paperwork and all, with Klau's name on it as the party's frontman (mostly as a joke). Garnet is helping run it.
- Captain [[Azure “Hop”|Hop]] flirted with her on the voyage to Hjirolis.
