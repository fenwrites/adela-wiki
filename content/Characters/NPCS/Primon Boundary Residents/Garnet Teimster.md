## Summary
A tiefling from [[Primon Boundary]] with a bad leg from a childhood break, and a good friend of the party, who helped her escape her small town. She sailed with them to [[Hjirolis]] (finding her sea legs quickly) and stayed in [[Ceropolis]] to learn the merchant trade with Tharr's backing.

## Activities and Rumours
- She invested in a carpenter supplying carts and made back her losses. "Klau's Quills" is in business.
- She sewed each of the Primon three a patch: a three-claw signature for Klau, a tree for Delrek and a scale for Tharr.
