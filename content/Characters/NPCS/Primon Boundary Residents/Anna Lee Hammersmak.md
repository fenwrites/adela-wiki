*Delrek's mother, who runs the Primon Inn*

## Summary
[[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]]'s mother, who runs the Primon Inn in [[Primon Boundary]]. A single mother, she married [[Sterling Starlight II]], Klau's father. She treats [[Klau]] as her own son, since he ate at her table every week. Their daughter, Samira, is half-sister to Klau and Delrek.

## Activities and Rumours
- Her letter told the boys that Samira was sick with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]]. The family took her to a Temple of Growth in [[Wardensport]].
- She reminds Delrek to change his underwear.
