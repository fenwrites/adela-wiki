*Hard-working researcher in Primon Boundary that feels out of their depth*

#npc 

## Appearance and Quirks
Rail-thin, with shoulder-length hair, glasses and somewhat bad posture. Kind, but a bit awkward.

## Summary
An independent researcher, a scholar of the [[Shimmerwyld]] who got swept up in the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] crisis because it falls in his field of study. When [[Finch]] and [[Nadra]] were children, he taught them the basics of magic.

## Activities and Rumours
- At the summit in [[Primon Boundary]], he shared his theory that chaos magic in the Shimmerwyld increases steadily in one direction, toward the old cultist fort, and sent the party to investigate Pollard Farms.
- The party could have gone with him to check out the old fort, but chose to go to [[Hjirolis]] instead.
- According to the latest letter from home, he has been off in the Shimmerwyld for weeks.
