*Traditional and honourable leader of primon boundary*


## Appearance and Quirks
[[Audric Malikan]] is an emerald dragonborn (shiny forest green scales) around 40 years old. They are tall, powerfully built, with a crested face. When rarely making official appearances, they are wearing full plate armor made of steel with tailoring and [[Primon boundary]]'s crest. Usually they wait simple but well-made clothing.
## Summary
[[Audric Malikan]] is the current Lord of [[Primon Boundary]]. 

## Relationships
- [[Cormac Malikan]]: Audric's Son
- [[Sorhan Malikan]]: Audric's Father and former Lord of [[Primon Boundary]]
## Activities and Rumours
- Audric has been opposed to further cutting back the [[Shimmerwyld]], citing his father Sorhan's wishes. This stance has been largely respected, but the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] has made much of [[Saers]] prefer to cut into the [[Shimmerwyld]]. Audric continues to resist these calls.
- Audric was poisoned when dealing with bandits near [[Saers]]. He nearly died, but [[Delrek Hammersmak]] managed to correctly identify the poison as Nether Flower, a purple flower found only deep underground.
