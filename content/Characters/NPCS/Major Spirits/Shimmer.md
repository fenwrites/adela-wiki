*A non-divine being of nature that possesses considerable power*

Shimmer first came to [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] in the [[Shimmerwyld]] as a shape of ever-moving vines, vaguely feminine in form, with an alien voice that seemed to echo inside his mind. It offered him the power to save the dying boy [[Ralof and Bjor|Bjor]], in exchange for access to his very being ([[Session 5 Recap|Session 5]]).

That power has only grown in him since, and in strange ways. Delrek has been hurt by trying to cut a tree in the Shimmerwyld, and he has manifested vines that look just like Shimmer's own.

Its goals and desires are unknowable. It seems somewhat benevolent, if chaotic.
