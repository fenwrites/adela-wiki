*Embodiment of nature*


For the legendary tree of the same name, see Arshanta (Tree)

## Appearance and Quirks
There are no known physical depictions of Arshanta.
## Summary
Arshanta is possibly a Divine, incredibly powerful mortal, or another god-level or greater being that is said to have brought nature to [[Adela]]. She is considered central to or the source of [[Shimmerwyld]], and part of her is said to be the driving force behind the massive world tree of the same name. It is unclear if Arshanta is an active participant in the events of the world, a reactive force in the form of the [[Shimmerwyld]], a passive observer, or even real, though it is implied that the [[Shimmerwyld]] itself reacted when the [[Inseore Gnomes]] originally attempted to tame the area.
## Relationships
- [[Inseore Gnomes]]: Worshipped by the [[Inseore Gnomes]], though degrees of faith are widely varied, and some focus on worshipping the tree or the [[Shimmerwyld]] itself.
- [[Shimmerwyld]]: The magical sprawling forest biome that is said to originate from a piece of Arshanta.
## Activities and Rumours
- [[Florence Glimmerglade]] described Arshanta with female pronouns and as a 'being' that 'has always been aware, sending a part of themself unto [[Adela]] in a great flash'. She also shared a unique sylvan word directly linked to Arshanta: 'Nymsharil' (Gave birth, grafted, granted sentience, cut off a piece of yourself).
- [[Delrek Hammersmak]] seems to have some connection to Arshanta, possibly through [[Shimmer]].
