## Doctrine
Sickness and injury can be understood and treated, and caring for the body is sacred work.

## In Ankarnassas
Three orders serve this Divine: Iasis Pharmakon (alchemy and preventative care), Iasis Chirurgeon (major health crises) and Iasis Charatus (community health and outreach). See [[Ankarnassas]].
