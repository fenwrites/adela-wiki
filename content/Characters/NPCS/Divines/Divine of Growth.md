## Summary
Called the Boundless or the Unbound. Temples of Growth often depict violent scenes from history, a reminder that destruction and growth are intertwined ("She grows for our sins"). Followers meet on the solstices. [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] serve Growth.

## Doctrine
Cyclical death and rebirth, creating something new, increasing production, having children, expanding property/nations, more crops, mindfulness.

## Notes
Has many different sects, and is sanctioned in [[Saers]]. The Divine of Fertility, once thought by some to be separate, is now understood to be the same Divine.
