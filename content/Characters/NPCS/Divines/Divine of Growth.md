## Summary
Called the Boundless or the Unbound. Temples of Growth often depict violent scenes from history, a reminder that destruction and growth are intertwined ("She grows for our sins"). Followers meet on the solstices. [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] serve Growth.
