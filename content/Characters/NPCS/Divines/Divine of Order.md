## Summary
One of the two patron Divines of [[Hjirolis]]. In [[Ceropolis]] it is remembered as Apolus, God-Emperor of the once great nation of the Isiliosi, whose tenets still guide Hjirolis: *"Order is a shield against chaos. The strong must guide the weak. The law is eternal and immutable."* A stained-glass window in Ceropolis shows the Great Binding, when Apolus created the Chain of Accord binding the tribes to one another. The Isiliosi ultimately fell, but Hjirolis holds that the failure was humanity's, not the Divine's.

## Apolus
Amid violent debates in the agora of [[Ankarnassas]] over war with the Sjuvven, the Divine of Order appeared floating twenty feet above the crowd: golden-skinned, clean-shaven, in a plain white toga, with a voice that silenced everyone. It named itself Apolus and issued an edict: *"Those that seek to foster Chaos have no right within Hjirolis. This nation is built on the foundation of Formation, and by my hand will civilization be restored."* Two weeks of celebrations were ordained across the nation.

## Branches of the Church
- **Ordinis Domos**: the largest, most traditional and strictest branch, which revealed Apolus. Its seat stands in Elikos, in [[Ankarnassas]].
- **Ordo Concordiae**: consensus-seeking mediators and lovers of debate, now in disarray.
