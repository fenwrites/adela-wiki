## Summary
One of the two patron Divines of [[Hjirolis]]. In [[Ceropolis]] it is remembered as Apolus, God-Emperor of the once great nation of the Isiliosi, whose tenets still guide Hjirolis: *"Order is a shield against chaos. The strong must guide the weak. The law is eternal and immutable."* A stained-glass window in Ceropolis shows the Great Binding, when Apolus created the Chain of Accord binding the tribes to one another. The Isiliosi ultimately fell, but Hjirolis holds that the failure was humanity's, not the Divine's.

## Apolus
Amid violent debates in the agora of [[Ankarnassas]] over war with the Sjuvven, the Divine of Order appeared floating twenty feet above the crowd: golden-skinned, clean-shaven, in a plain white toga, with a voice that silenced everyone. It named itself Apolus and issued an edict: *"Those that seek to foster Chaos have no right within Hjirolis. This nation is built on the foundation of Formation, and by my hand will civilization be restored."* Forty days of celebrations were ordained across the nation, capped by the marathon around Lake Atmos.

Apolus then moved over the lake above Ankar to address the whole city, and a silver light was seen across all the tiers. The waters of the lake and river turned silver for a day (a miracle, the common folk say), and the year, which had looked set to be the rainiest and most flood-prone on record, turned to good weather. It still rains, but nothing like it did. Like all Divines, Apolus is ungendered, though it appears as a man. Apolus has done little directly since then, and is said to be in seclusion at the Ordinis Domos headquarters, so nobody can see them right now.

## Branches of the Church
- **Ordinis Domos**: the most traditional and strictest branch, which revealed Apolus. Historically one of the smaller branches, it has swelled with converts since the revelation and is fast becoming the largest. Its seat stands in Elikos, in [[Ankarnassas]].
- **Ordinis Concordiae**: consensus-seeking mediators and lovers of debate, now in disarray.
- **Ordinis Cosmos**: devoted to heavenly order, the order of the natural world.

## Doctrine
Strict hierarchies or methods of operation; the scientific method.

## Notes
A primary Divine of [[Hjirolis]]. "Stifler" is a derogatory nickname.
