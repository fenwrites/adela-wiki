## About the Divines
The creators of the world, known as Divines, are widely known to exist. Very rarely, they walk among mortals as leaders, warlords or esoteric wanderers with inscrutable goals. Most people have received, or at least heard of, their blessings, and most are polytheistic, devoting themselves to one or two major Divines.

No one knows exactly how many Divines there are. Their domains range from War and Deceit to Rabbits and Woodworking. "Divine of ____" is the proper form, but people often use nicknames (for example, in some regions whiskers are lucky, so the Divine of Luck becomes "Mr./Mrs. Whiskers").
