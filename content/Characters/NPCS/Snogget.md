*Cute, intelligent, and passive companion of Illesara*

[[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]'s fey companion, in a tiny wizard's hat. His coat is brown in summer and white in winter. Illy prefers him brown, and so does he: whenever he's in his white coat, he does his best to get dirty. He has really weirdly long whiskers.

He's friendly to [[The Party|the party]], but pretty attached to Illy.
