*Deceptive cultist*

A cultist leader the party captured at [[Almurbalarammi]] ([[Session 12 Recap|Session 12]]), a human woman in a black robe with a red stripe along the edge, with ZZ tattoos on both wrists.

She claimed to be a poor, confused girl from [[Oranos]], with parents named [[Gregori Ostensa|Gregori]] and [[Siri Ostensa|Siri]]. The party didn't buy it. She slipped free and ran back to the ruin, where [[Klau]] killed her with a frost bolt ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]).
