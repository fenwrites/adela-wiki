*Well-loved travelling chef*


## Appearance and Quirks
Argus "Cooky" Surs is an older portly Orc man, mostly just wears his greasy cook clothes.
## Summary
Argus Cooky is a **ship cook** operating out of HES Longsail.  He usually goes by his nickname 'Cooky', and is very friendly. He has a wealth of knowledge on the Southmoon Sea, and loves telling stories. He is a bit of a legend among sailors in the region.
## Relationships
- [[Azure “Hop”]]: Argus' Captain.

## Activities and Rumours
- About seven feet tall and rotund, with short stubby tusks and a friar's ring of hair. Grumbles about his joints, but would rather die than quit.
