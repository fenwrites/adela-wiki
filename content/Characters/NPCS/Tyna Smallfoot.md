*A travelling and friendly halfling messenger*

#npc

## Appearance and Quirks
Very short even for a halfling, slim, with short honey-brown hair and large eyes. From a distance she could pass for a young boy, but up close she is clearly in her late thirties or early forties. She has several small scars on her left arm.

## Summary
A messenger returning to [[Miskral]] aboard the [[HES Longsail]]. She is friendly to everyone and an experienced sailor who helps the crew for fun, but deflects personal questions and always keeps her packages on her.

## Activities and Rumours
- She invited the party to visit her in the town of Warrensburrow, in Miskral.
