*War-loving leader of the shimmerwyld invasion*

## Summary
Zanangathor is a fiend tied to the cultists operating in the [[Shimmerwyld]] and to the [[Infernal In the Shimmerwyld|infernal incursions]] there.

## Activities and Rumours
- At [[Almurbalarammi]], the party found the body of a devil, killed by a Couatl that had raided the site before they arrived. The body bore a Z symbol, and inside it was a smashed green gemstone.

  ![[Zanangathor's Mark.png|120]]
- [[Rami Ostensa]], the cultist leader the party captured there, mentioned Zanangathor in reference to that Z. She had ZZ tattoos on both wrists.
