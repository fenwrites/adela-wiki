*Dealer of souls, merchant king of the hells*

The holder of [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]'s contract. When Tharr's request for a review was approved, he met Murhz in a dream ([[Session 15 Recap|Session 15]]): a massive, overweight devil behind a desk buried in papers, dressed in an opulent suit and gold jewellery, with a bowler cap and razor-sharp white teeth.

Murhz came off as friendly at first, but quickly turned deadly serious, and violent, when annoyed. His imp assistant, [[Bip]], struck Tharr with a psychic attack on command.
