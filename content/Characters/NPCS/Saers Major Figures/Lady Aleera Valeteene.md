## Appearance and Quirks
A tall, imposing dragonborn with deep sapphire scales and many short, pointed spines along her face. She travels with her stoic bodyguard, Shamac, also a sapphire dragonborn.

## Activities and Rumours
- She sailed to [[Hjirolis]] aboard the HES Longsail, unhappy with the accommodations. She enjoys conversation, as long as she's the one steering it.
- She is in Hjir to marry Austrus Antoniiadas, joining her family's iron operations with the [[Antoniiadas Family]]'s business.
