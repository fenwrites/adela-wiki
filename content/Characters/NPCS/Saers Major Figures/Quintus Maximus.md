## Appearance and Quirks
Short and square: hairy, broad and muscular, with short black hair, a goatee and a square jaw. He moves very quickly when he needs to.

## Summary
A Hjir envoy returning home aboard the HES Longsail. He keeps to himself, speaks with a thick Hjir accent, and says he is visiting family.
