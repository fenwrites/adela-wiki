*Shimmerwyld researcher for Saers' government*

#npc 
[[Saers]]

## Summary
Augustus Fearly is the lead researcher studying the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]], with [[Klau]] and [[Waylan Wiles]] among his apprentices. He is a notable figure in [[Saers]] with Hjir ancestry.

## Activities and Rumours
- At the summit in [[Primon Boundary]] he backed [[Orlean Vetiver]]'s call for a War Mantle.
- He is part of The People's Coalition, a group working to increase the Empire's power over local governments.
- He urged the party to bring their findings to Saers. When they took [[Morro Shanderby|Morro]]'s mission to [[Hjirolis]] instead, he sent his messenger, [[Fibbart Miller]], along with them.
