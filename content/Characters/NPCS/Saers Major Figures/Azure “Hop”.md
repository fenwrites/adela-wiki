*Former pirate turned navy captain*

#npc 
[[Saers]]

## Appearance and Quirks
A pale red tiefling woman, tall and well built, with a wooden peg leg that barely slows her down.

## Summary
Captain of the HES Longsail ("Her Empress's Service"), a sleek cog whose long single sail she proudly helped design. She sailed the party from [[Wardensport]] to [[Pelethra]]. All business while the ship is working, she relaxes into stories and jokes once all is well, and commands her crew's respect through both leadership and friendship. She lost her leg to a harpy, which sheared it off and flew away eating it, and has hated harpies ever since.
