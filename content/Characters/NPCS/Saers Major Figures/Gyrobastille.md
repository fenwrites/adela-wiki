## Summary
Gyrobastille, "Gyro", is [[Morro Shanderby]]'s informant in [[Oranos]], where she ran a teahouse called Aromatica that was really a brothel, until it was shut down.

## Appearance and Quirks
She appears as a woman of moderate height with a dignified beauty: bald, young-looking, of an age that's hard to guess. She smiles, swears and laughs a lot. Her staff at Aromatica included Denoument ("call me Dane"), Lark, Laurel, Thalia, Little Owl and Brine.

## Activities and Rumours
- She told the party how the Divine of Order revealed itself as Apolus in the agora of [[Ankarnassas]], and that the next day all brothels were ordered shut as "locuses of disorder".
- She named contacts in Ankarnassas: Ionyra Asterides (a former brothel-keeper with fingers in many pots), Tamias Inices (of the Koinon Nyktos, a patriot but no fan of Order, and her ex-lover), Syndo Tettigon (advisor to Archon Layeisto, who believes the Shimmerwyld border should stay exactly as it is) and Kaynon Oriados (a level-headed senior [[Fatebinders|Fatebinder]]).
