[[The Party]]
[[Morro Shanderby]]



## Summary
Gyrobastille, "Gyro", is [[Morro Shanderby]]'s informant in [[Oranos]], where she ran a teahouse called Aromatica that was really a brothel, until it was shut down.

## Appearance and Quirks
As the proprietor of Aromatica she appeared as a tall woman of about thirty, with shoulder-length hair and a dignified beauty. She smiles, swears and laughs a lot: boisterous, straightforward, charming and witty, fond of a long, drawling *"darling"*, and quick to poke fun at naivety. The party has seen her change shape. Her staff at Aromatica included Denoument ("call me Dane"), a bald, extremely handsome half-elf of about twenty, Lark, Laurel, Thalia, Little Owl and Brine.

## Activities and Rumours
- She told the party how the Divine of Order revealed itself as Apolus in the agora of [[Ankarnassas]], and that the next day all brothels were ordered shut as "locuses of disorder".
- She named contacts in Ankarnassas: Ionyra Asterides (a former brothel-keeper with fingers in many pots), Tamias Inices (of the Koinon Nyktos, a patriot but no fan of Order, and her ex-lover), Syndo Tettigon (advisor to Archon Layeisto, who believes the Shimmerwyld border should stay exactly as it is) and Kaynon Oriados (a level-headed senior [[Fatebinders|Fatebinder]]).

- After Aromatica was shut down, she left [[Oranos]] for [[Miskral]] on her own business, explaining that she could keep an eye on the situation in [[Hjirolis]] from afar. She told the party the changes in Oranos were happening all across Hjirolis.

- She left her assistant Denoument in [[Oranos]] to keep gathering information.
