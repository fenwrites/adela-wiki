*Drug- and fun-loving surprisingly competent diplomat*

#npc
[[Saers]]
[[The Party]]

## Appearance and Quirks
An average-looking human man with short brown hair and brown eyes, who smiles all the time. He is the life of the party and always seems to have some alcohol or drug on him (he once handed out ten doses of "yayo").

## Summary
Fibbart is a messenger in [[Augustus Fearly]]'s employ who travelled with the party to [[Hjirolis]] as Saers' envoy. He plays the part of a bumbling, likeable envoy, but is far more competent than he lets on, and shifts his manner to suit whoever he is talking to.

## Activities and Rumours
- He presented the party to Archon [[Aoran Aurelius]] in [[Ceropolis]] with a chest of gemstones worth around 50,000 gold.
- He stayed in Ceropolis to negotiate, hoping to set up a proper embassy, and has been a friend to [[Garnet Teimster|Garnet]] there.
