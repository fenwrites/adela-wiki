*Neutral-toned cleric to Community*


## Appearance and Quirks
Amansti is a middle-aged amethyst dragonborn. She wears dark blue vestments of the [[Divine of Community]], including a white headband marking her rank. She carries a simple wooden staff.
## Summary
Amansti Warbler is a **priest of Community** operating out of [[Saers (City)|Saers]].
