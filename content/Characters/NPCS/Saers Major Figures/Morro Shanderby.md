## Summary
Her Holiness' Justice Morro Shanderby, a halfling woman, is the Empress's Justice in [[Saers]]. She presided over the summit in [[Primon Boundary]] about clearing the [[Shimmerwyld]], and granted two weeks before a vote. She says she works for the people, and she knew [[Sorhan Malikan]] well.

## Activities and Rumours
- She sent [[Klau]], [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] to [[Hjirolis]] as her messengers, carrying a package to Archon [[Aoran Aurelius]] in [[Ceropolis]], then to gather word about the Shimmerwyld and meet her informant, [[Gyrobastille]], in [[Oranos]]. They were told never to mention Gyrobastille to [[Fibbart Miller]].
- She makes no secret of her dislike for The People's Coalition, the group behind [[Augustus Fearly]] and [[Orlean Vetiver]], though how deep it runs is unclear.
- Her seal, a set of scales, is recognised by guards across Saers.
- She was exasperated that [[Augustus Fearly]] found out about the mission and attached his own man to it.
- She later sent a letter relieving the three of their duty to Saers, after they abused their diplomatic position in Hjirolis.
