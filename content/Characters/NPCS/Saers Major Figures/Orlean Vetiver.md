*Intelligent, scheming, and influential member of Saers' government and Brussaer*

#npc 

## Appearance and Quirks
A blue dragonborn: self-important, intelligent, calculating and observant.

## Summary
Lord Orlean of the Vetiver Clan, an advisor to Saers' imperial court, sent to [[Primon Boundary]] to deal with [[Audric Malikan|Audric]]'s refusal to clear the [[Shimmerwyld]]. He sits on the faculty and board of directors of [[Brussaer]], and is part of The People's Coalition.

## Activities and Rumours
- At the summit he proposed a War Mantle to strip Audric of his authority.
- He sneered at Audric "up in the hills trying to reclaim his glory days while the undead rise at his border."
