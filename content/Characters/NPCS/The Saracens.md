*A family living in a dark, rocky otherworld, who speak to Klau through the Chronicle*

## Appearance and Quirks
- **Lysander Saracen**: a black-haired elf astronomer.
- **Corrina Saracen**: his human wife, who made the watch the family keeps time by.
- **Elowen Saracen**: their half-elf daughter, about ten, who had never seen a dragonborn.

## Summary
A family living in a cabin in a dark, endless, rocky place they call the Nothing. [[Klau]] first glimpsed them in a vision at [[Almurbalarammi]], studying with [[The Chronicle]] open on their table ([[Session 12 Recap|Session 12]]). Elowen later began writing to him through the book ([[Session 14 Recap|Session 14]]). 

## Activities and Rumours
- While bewitched by the harpy at [[Bastion XIII]], Klau met the whole family ([[Session 22 Recap|Session 22]]). They said they had lived there for thirty years while three centuries passed outside. They warned that entities of great destruction, the [[List of Divines|Divines]], are sealed away in the stars, that the seal is weakening, and that the Divines are fated to return within seven years.
