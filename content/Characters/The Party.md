Five adventurers of growing renown, currently stuck in Thema Pende, the lowest tier of [[Ankarnassas]], while they wait for the papers to go higher.

## How They Came Together
[[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]], [[Klau]] and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] grew up together in [[Primon Boundary]], and stumbled into the [[Shimmerwyld]] during the Choosing, their trial for adulthood. Years later they reunited as the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] worsened, and [[Saers]] sent them to investigate the far side of the forest, in [[Hjirolis]], alongside the envoy [[Fibbart Miller]].

In [[Ceropolis]], [[Mona]] dropped out of the ceiling into their meeting with the Archon. In the eastern Shimmerwyld they met [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]], who brought them home to the [[Glimmerglade]]. Together, the five escorted the [[Inseore Gnomes|gnomes]] out of the forest as infernal cultists closed in. They have since lost their role as Saers' official representatives, and have come to Ankarnassas looking for answers.
