*Reclusive woman protecting her people and seeking to understand magics*

#player_character

> A life in the [[Shimmerwyld]] leaves you with a different perspective on light and darkness. Many common folk see shadows as danger, a place for creatures to hide and hunt. Illy knows that shadows are not just the lack of light, but home to an entire world beneath the canopy, filled with animals, plants and spirits few outsiders will ever understand. Deep in this place shines a light like no other, upon a tree that many claim gave birth to the world herself. There she found peace, but something made of neither darkness nor light disturbed it. A feeling of disquiet fell over [[Arshanta's Grove]], and with it came destruction. The [[Inseore Gnomes]], long guardians of the Shimmerwyld, have begun their evacuation against a threat of unknown scale. After meeting the Primon three, who helped save her people, she was guided onward with them, connected to a glowing gem unlike any she has seen, with a being inside asking for her help. Keeping it alive with her arcane energies, she returns to the very place where she learned to manipulate magic: [[Ankarnassas]].

## Background
A gnome of the [[Glimmerglade]], daughter of the schoolteacher [[Loette Glimmerglade]] and the artificer [[Thibido Stonyglade]]. The Glimmerglade teaches its children to avoid their ancestors' mistakes: do not try to assert dominion over nature. Understand it, coexist with it, and in that you may find peace. She was born under the [[Star Signs|Faerie]] star sign. She learned her magic in [[Ankarnassas]], at the Lyceum of Alcion, as apprentice to [[Mr. Hannely]], a gnome teacher who recruited her on a visit to the [[Shimmerwyld]]. She eventually left to explore her own way, heading deep into the Shimmerwyld before meeting the party.

## Right Now in Ankarnassas
- The [[Green Gemstone|green gemstone]] is her main priority.
- She also wants to help her family and the [[Inseore Gnomes]], who are being thrust into Hjirran politics.
