*Intense man in battle for his own soul*

#player_character

> Balance, a flipped coin landing on its edge. Balance, found in a book that documents transactions, recording the power of wealth changing hands. Balance, found on the slippery rocks between the tidepools of the Primon Boundary delta. A single glance at an infernal document has offset Tharr's balance. Soulbound to an unjust and judicial demonic entity, punished for unwittingly stumbling upon something he should not know, Tharr lives life with a mark on his head. But even off balance, there are alliances to be made, wealth to accrue and power to be gained. Who can Tharr trust? [[Bip]], a seemingly friendly yet conniving imp working for his mortal enemy? His friends, who give him sidelong glances as he becomes more and more single-minded? Himself, who throws a coin hoping it won't land on tails? A merchant learns to take only calculated risks, but will Tharr resist the urge to gamble with his conscience to save his soul, or is it already too late?

## Background
A [[Naltese Peoples|Naltese]] bugbear from the delta around [[Primon Boundary]]. On the night before the Choosing he undertook Naltechikakua, the Naltese adulthood rite, overseen by [[Mahisakueu]], and chose the At'Aka (Tidewalk). In the cave in the [[Shimmerwyld]] he read an infernal note, resisted its malice, and has carried a mark ever since. Afterwards he studied mercantilism like his father did at [[Brussaer]] in [[Saers (City)]] alongside [[Klau]].

## The Story So Far
- **The Choosing** ([[Session 2 Recap|Sessions 2]]–[[Session 3 Recap|3]]): the Tidewalk rite, and the infernal note in the cave.
- **The road to Hjirolis** ([[Session 6 Recap|6]]): hired [[Odd Job]] in [[Wardensport]] for a one-off job of his own.
- **Ceropolis** ([[Session 8 Recap|8]]): [[Arranos]], a monk working for infernal powers, warned him that the consequences of his actions were coming, and left him unconscious.
- **Escorting the gnomes** ([[Session 15 Recap|15]]): grew fixated on the green gemstone. In his dreams he was granted a review of his contract and met [[Murhz’egellaghorighhtz|Murhz]] and his imp, Bip, and talked Bip into an understanding: *"You scratch my back and I'll scratch yours."*
- **Hjirolis** ([[Session 18 Recap|18]]–[[Session 23 Recap|23]]): hunted down Ezolio in [[Loxos]] as a favour to Bip. In [[Oranos]] he spent half a day getting a horse permit for Vauve.
