One moment you are in a frantic struggle with cultists in [[Almurbalarammi]]. The next, you are waking up in a rocky, dark, and barren environment. It’s cold, even for you. As you explore this space, you become aware of a faint yellow light nearby, distinct among the endless rocky terrain. As you approach it, you realize the light is from a window of a small, quaint wood cabin. Approaching the window, you look inside to see the following scene:  
  
A middle-aged black-haired elf and woman occupy the central space, and from their actions they are clearly a couple. Both of them have black hair, darker complexions, and are simply dressed in the robes of scholars. They playfully cook a meal together, chopping herbs and other ingredients you can’t quite see. Around them are several work benches with seemingly-endless stacks of well-organized books, scrolls, and loose parchment. On one of the tables, something catches your eye: A thick, brown-leather bound tome lies open on the table. [[The Chronicle]] is sitting open on their table.

  

Suddenly, the face of a young girl appears at the window. She shrieks, a muffled noise through the glass paned window. Both of the adults turn toward you, and lock eyes for a moment. You watch as one says something, and with an arm motion you are thrown back 30 feet from the house. As you land on the rocky ground you suddenly jerk awake, back in [[Almurbalarammi]].
