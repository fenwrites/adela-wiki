*Ostentatious man figuring out his own story*

#player_character

> To know the past is to understand the present and predict the future. Being a dragonborn in [[Saers]] means knowing you have some link to a creature of legend, the great dragon [[Malikan]]. Klau's innocent interest in fables was shattered in the [[Shimmerwyld]], where legend and nightmare came to life. Learning to channel the magical power of stories themselves, Klau stumbled into Bert's Books one day, a seemingly innocent bookstore in [[Wardensport]]. There he found (or perhaps was found by) [[The Chronicle]], a blank book that would nonetheless tell a story more powerful than any he had read before. It seems linked to a strange place with people inside, who gave a foreboding warning: within seven years, the Divines locked within the stars will return. With little understanding or time to question, Klau is placed in the position of the unknowing protagonist, but can he even find the beginning of the story before it's too late?

## Background
Full name Sterling Starlight III, son of the tailor [[Sterling Starlight II]], though he goes by Klau. He was born under the light of the blue star [[Shalar]]. His father married [[Anna Lee Hammersmak]], making [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] his stepbrother. After the Choosing he studied at [[Brussaer]] in [[Saers (City)]] with [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]], and he is a seanchaí of the [[Seanchai Guild]], the Keepers of Past and Present.

## The Story So Far
- **The Choosing** ([[Session 2 Recap|Sessions 2]]–[[Session 3 Recap|3]]): his father handed him a commission for ceremonial robes, to be made alongside rival tailor [[Isadora]].
- **The road to Hjirolis** ([[Session 6 Recap|6]]): found [[The Chronicle]] at Bert's Books in Wardensport.
- **Ceropolis** ([[Session 8 Recap|8]]): became involved with the [[Divine of History]].
- **Almurbalarammi** ([[Session 12 Recap|12]]–[[Session 14 Recap|14]]): knocked unconscious, he dreamt of a cabin in a dark, rocky place where a family studied with the Chronicle open on their table ([[Session 12 Recap#Klau's Vision|Klau's Vision]]). He finished off [[Rami Ostensa]] with a frost bolt. Soon after, a girl named Elowen Saracen began writing to him in the Chronicle.
- **Escorting the gnomes** ([[Session 15 Recap|15]]): soothed Tharr through his infernal dreams, and helped Henrietta Sunglade tailor robes.
- **Oranos** ([[Session 20 Recap|20]]–[[Session 22 Recap|22]]): a nightmare took hold of his soul and walked him out to [[Bastion XIII]], where a harpy had bewitched him to bring her the Chronicle. In an ethereal place he met the Saracen family, who warned that the Divines sealed in the stars will return within seven years.
