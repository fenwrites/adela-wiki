*Small-town guy torn between two gods*

#player_character

> After years of faith to [[Divine of Growth|The Boundless]], a chance encounter with [[Shimmer]], a mysterious botanical being, granted him the power to [[Ralof and Bjor|save a child's life]]. Since then he has been pulled in two directions: the orderly cycle of death and rebirth he has studied for years, or the new chaotic powers that have rapidly taken root within him. His altruistic interest in helping others has already clashed with his destructive power, leaving a wake of admirers and a name, "The Disembowler". Increasingly battle-hardened, scarred and famous, can he find peace to unravel his own human life as he drifts further from the family he holds dear in Primon?

## Background
Delrek grew up in [[Primon Boundary]], where his mother, [[Anna Lee Hammersmak|Anna Lee]], ran the Primon Inn. His father is unknown. She eventually married [[Sterling Starlight II]], making [[Klau]] his stepbrother, and gave birth to their sister Samira. After the party's disastrous trip into the [[Shimmerwyld]] during the Choosing, Delrek stayed home to apprentice under the dwarf priest [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] as an acolyte of the Divine of Growth, while Klau and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] left for [[Brussaer]].

Delrek was born under the Lady star sign.

## The Story So Far
- **The Choosing** ([[Session 1 Recap|Sessions 1]]–[[Session 3 Recap|3]]): a tense family dinner, the exams, and the cave where he tried to pull [[Garnet Teimster|Garnet]] away from a devil.
- **Primon Boundary, years later** ([[Session 4 Recap|4]]–[[Session 5 Recap|5]]): took part in the summit, identified the poison that nearly killed Lord [[Audric Malikan]], and walked into the Shimmerwyld for guidance. There Shimmer offered him the power to cure the dying boy Bjor in exchange for access to his very being. He accepted.
- **Ceropolis** ([[Session 8 Recap|8]]): saved a boy named Augies from harpies, and his reputation began to spread.
- **The Shimmerwyld** ([[Session 11 Recap|11]]–[[Session 15 Recap|15]]): cured himself and [[Mona]] of the blight, cleansed a root shrine to Arshanta, and saw a vision of cultists closing on the [[Sunglade]]. During the earthquake he tapped into Shimmer and partially transformed in front of his friends to shield the gnome caravan. [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] took a keen interest in him as someone who holds both Divine and the power of Shimmer.
- **Hjirolis** ([[Session 16 Recap|16]]–[[Session 23 Recap|23]]): a letter from home brought news that Samira was sick with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]]. In [[Ankarnassas]] he met the local chapter of the Divine of Growth.

## Relationships
- [[Klau]]: stepbrother and lifelong friend; the two have always had a pretty smooth relationship.
- [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]: lifelong friend. Delrek is very supportive of Tharr and his current soul problems, but may be beginning to hesitate with his changing friend.
- [[Mona]]: Delrek is the most suspicious of the strange girl, though they have some camaraderie in their innocence.
- [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]: Delrek played a significant role in saving her family and people, but cast Command on her over the green gemstone, though he did eventually return it to her.
- [[Anna Lee Hammersmak]]: his mother.
- [[Adoniram Gromsh]]: his mentor in the faith of Growth, who writes to him in Dwarvish.
- [[Shimmer]]: the botanical being whose power now grows inside him.
- [[Garnet Teimster]]: childhood friend; she sewed him a tree patch.

- A veritable array of weaponry.
