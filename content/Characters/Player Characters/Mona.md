*Rebel girl with a penchant for spirits*

#player_character

> [[Spirits]]: the things only Mona could see. For most of her life, that was the truth she lived with. Growing up in a wealthy house in [[Ankarnassas]], she counted them as friends, small strange creatures in the corners that no one else noticed. Adults called them imaginary. Other children called them proof she was a freak. Gold could buy a great many things, but not someone who believed her. So she left home young to find answers, and the spirits came with her: an evolving arrangement of focused emotions shaped into powerful magic, though what she feels is not always what she would choose. Then, deep in the [[Shimmerwyld]], the ground shifted beneath her. The [[Inseore Gnomes|Inseore gnomes]] didn’t just believe her. They saw the spirits too, named them, studied them, even knew which ones love cheese. After a lifetime of being the only one, Mona was suddenly one of many. And a stranger named [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] looked at her and named her [[Vedma]], whatever that meant. Since then she has taken up a black censer whose green flame whispers something only she can hear, and a letter from home has found her at last. Now she has returned to Ankarnassas, a city the [[Divine of Order]] has claimed as its own. She left with nothing but questions. Is she ready for the answers waiting at home?

## Background
A rebellious young human with a penchant for [[Spirits]], who sees and speaks with them where others see nothing.

## The Story So Far
- **Ceropolis** ([[Session 7 Recap|Session 7]]): dropped unexpectedly into the party's meeting in the Acropolis. How she got in is still a mystery.
- **The Glimmerglade** ([[Session 10 Recap|10]]–[[Session 11 Recap|11]]): rejoined the group, caught the blight, and was cured by [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]].
- **The Sunglade** ([[Session 14 Recap|14]]): [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] took an interest in her as someone potentially possessing spirit powers.
- **Escorting the gnomes** ([[Session 15 Recap|15]]): questioned everyone about their pasts. Nervous about [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]'s fixation on the green gemstone, she handed it to him, starting a rift with [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]].
- **Bastion XIII** ([[Session 22 Recap|22]]): claimed the harpy leader's black spiked censer, which burns with green fire and smoke in her hands.
