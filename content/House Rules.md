*The table's gritty realism rules*

## Gritty Realism
Gritty realism tempers the usual D&D power surge, where level 1 adventurers walk into the woods and, a few weeks and a few dozen encounters later, emerge as some of the strongest people in the land. 5e is balanced around 2–4 encounters a day; unless we're dungeon crawling, we'll rarely do more than one per session. Injuries feel a bit worse (a good thing), and if something is particularly bad we might roll for scars or broken bones.

- **Short rest:** an 8-hour sleep.
- **Long rest:** one week of light activity, with no encounters. Plan downtime if you like: study a language, gain proficiency with a weapon, research a topic, send letters. The DM may be flexible: if few resources were spent and you get a full day's rest before a big fight, it might count as a long rest.

## Spell Durations
| Standard 5e | Gritty Realism |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | 1 hour |
| 1 hour | 8 hours |
| 8 hours | 4 days |
| 24 hours | 7 days |
| 10 days | 30 days |

## Falling Unconscious
Falling unconscious three times in one fight leaves a lasting injury. Current injuries:
- [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]: permanent scarring on his leg and a noticeable limp (−5 ft movement speed)
- [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]]: a persistent cough (−1 Stealth)

If anything feels too strong, too weak or too fiddly, we'll adjust it, and if necessary walk it back to the normal rules.
