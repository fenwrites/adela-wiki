*The party's own company, with Klau's name on it as a joke and Tharr's paperwork behind it*

## Overview
A recently established business with a few small ventures back in [[Ceropolis]]. It's [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]'s push to make the party a proper company, paperwork and all, with [[Klau]]'s name on it as frontman. [[Garnet Teimster|Garnet]] helps run it from Ceropolis.

## Notable Members
- [[Klau]]: frontman.
- [[Tharr Brirr]]: the driving force.
- [[Delrek Hammersmak]]: founding member.
- [[Garnet Teimster]]: runs the day-to-day.

## What the Party Knows
- **The permit:** the party holds a **two-week temporary business permit**, which had about 1.5 weeks left when they arrived in [[Ankarnassas]]. After that, the party can't do business themselves until they re-apply, which is a simple matter. Tharr, this one's yours. Garnet is assumed to be on top of the main permits back in Ceropolis.
- **Ventures:** Garnet has invested in the timber line behind the carts that ship [[Antoniiadas Family|Antoniiadas]] goods: lumberjacks sell to the men who shape the wood, who sell to the carpenters who build the carts. She made back her losses, and a bit more.
- **Downtime:** because the paperwork is real, the party can do business under the name.
