[[Hjirolis]] is an Autocracy led by [[Autokrator Uluros IX]], with noble families holding land and influence beneath him. An estate's head is its Oikonomos, its ruling landlord (see [[Hjirolis#Political Structure|Political Structure]]).

## Known Families
- [[Antoniiadas Family]]: merchants, anti-Sjuvven, and strong proponents of order. Hard workers, respectable and rude.
- [[Calakos Family]]: pro-Sjuvven, recently disgraced, and exiled from Ankar.
- [[Euterpides Family]]: new and fun, patrons of modern art and culture.
- Geogetes: the largest family of the [[Divine of Order|Order]] faithful.
- Isokrates: the current Autokrator's family.
- [[Leoprepes Family]]: pompous new money, all about the gold.
- Mavrogheni: relatively unknown. *"Aren't those the monks living in the east? You don't hear much about them."*
- [[Mnemonides Family]]: boring and respectable, full of accountants, librarians and scholars.
- Oinopoios: the largest family of the [[Divine of History|History]] faithful, and the official record keepers for the city and its holy history.
- [[Orreni Family]]: agricultural, and not thought about much. Their estate lies near the edge of the [[Shimmerwyld]].
