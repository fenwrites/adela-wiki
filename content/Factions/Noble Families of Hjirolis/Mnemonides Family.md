*A stuffy house of accountants, librarians and scholars, devoted to the Divine of History*


## What Commoners Say
Boring. A respectable family whose ventures employ good folk, full of accountants, librarians and scholars. They have widespread links to the public amphitheatres.

## Activities and Rumours
- They are involved in the celebrations for [[Divine of Order|Apolus]], though the [[Euterpides Family]] is putting on most of them.
