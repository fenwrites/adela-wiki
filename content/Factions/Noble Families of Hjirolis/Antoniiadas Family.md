## What Commoners Say
Merchants, anti-Sjuvven, and strong proponents of order: hard workers, respectable, and rude. They have strong links to the bathhouses, and a mild presence in a few upper-tier amphitheatres (traditional debate and plays only). They are also involved in the celebrations for [[Divine of Order|Apolus]].
