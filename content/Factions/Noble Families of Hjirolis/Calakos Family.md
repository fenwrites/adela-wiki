## What Commoners Say
Pro-Sjuvven, likable but foolish. They were well liked before, and had strong links to the schools and hospitals. Many now outright hate them, though much of that is people jumping on the bandwagon. Nobody will say good things about them in public, for fear of being linked to them. The schools and hospitals aren't doing well as Calakos money dries up.

## Activities and Rumours
- The family's father was recently executed over the assassination attempt on [[Autokrator Uluros IX]], and the house has fallen from grace, in full exile from Ankar.
- Many folk think it might be a conspiracy, since the family was allowed to continue on at all.
