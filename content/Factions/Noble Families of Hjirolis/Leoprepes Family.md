*Fast-rising new money, all about the gold, and pompous with it*


## Overview
Somewhat new money. The Leoprepes have risen rapidly by making deals with new [[Sjuvven]] merchants when others wouldn't. They have a palace, as well as their Villa Ambrosine in [[Oranos]], and are of somewhat mixed blood.

## What Commoners Say
Pompous, unlikable, and all about the money. Hjirrans see them as impure and underhanded, but begrudgingly respect their reputation. Sjuvven see them as benefactors and, to some degree, as traitors. It depends who you ask.

## Members and Household
- The eldest son: pompous and blond, and sure he'll win the marathon.

## Activities and Rumours
- The eldest son is training for the marathon around Lake Atmos, running through the tiers with an escort. On the party's first day in [[Ankarnassas]] ([[Session 23 Recap|Session 23]]), his escort shoved the party out of the way, and he slapped [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] before leaving.
