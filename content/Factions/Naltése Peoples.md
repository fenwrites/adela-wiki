## Customs
- Naltechikakua, the adulthood rite, in which the young choose a spiritual path: Kamihak (Woodspath) or At'Aka (Tidewalk).
- Naltése incense: when lit, those who breathe it smell the sea, can hold their breath for an hour, and grow features to help them swim.

## Overview
Indigenous island and coastal bugbears who lived on [[Nosovae]] long before the current nations formed. The islands off the southeastern coast are their home, and their holy grounds include the island chain formed by the supervolcano [[Orronoké]]. Over the years their peoples have diverged, some making their way through war, some joining other nations, some forming their own ([[Svudek]]). They live life at a different pace than most (somewhat like [[Miskral]], whom they get along with), and they adapt well to living alongside expanding nations. Ultimately, though, their heart lies at sea.

Think of them as nomadic fishing people who have somewhat integrated into a few nations while keeping a nation of their own. They are dispersed, but share an identity and a connection to Orronoké. The bugbears along the southern coast, including the fishing village just southeast of the [[Hjirolis]] border, are all Naltése.
