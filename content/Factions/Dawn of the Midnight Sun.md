*Cultist group with inscrutable goals*

A cult with inscrutable goals, known to worship the blue star [[Shalar]]. [[Mona]] learned about them while questioning the party ([[Session 15 Recap|Session 15]]).

They had an outpost in the western [[Shimmerwyld]], the old fort [[Waylan Wiles]] was heading to when [[Klau]], [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] left by ship.

They may be connected to the cultists in [[Tollen Boundary]], and to Ezolio's recruiting of orphans in [[Loxos]]. That would mean they may also have links to the infernals.
