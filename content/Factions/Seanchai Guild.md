*Storytellers guild of saers*

#faction 
[[Saers]]

## Overview
Also known as the Keepers of Past and Present: a guild of storytellers and historians in [[Saers]] that preserves history by passing down stories. A member is called a seanchaí (pronounced "shawna-key"). [[Klau]] is one.
