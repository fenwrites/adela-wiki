*The officially sanctioned thieves of Ankarnassas, a frowned-upon part of doing business*

## Overview
The Kleptonomoi, "the lawful thieves", are the sanctioned thieves guild of [[Ankarnassas]]. A member is called a Kleptonomos. The guild has a long history and is somewhat integrated into proper society, less a shadowy underworld than a frowned-upon part of business. [[Hjirolis]] accepts that theft will happen regardless and prefers to regulate it, and the arrangement also helps keep feuds between rival noble houses in check.

They mirror the [[Moraktonoi]] and are rivals with them, and they keep a strict rule against killing.

## Hiring the Guild
- **Where:** the guild hall in the Rises, Thema Tessra, near the north wall. There is none in Thema Pende.
- **Who can hire them:** anyone with the right gold, and the right paperwork. The paperwork is called a work order.
- **Cost:** a basic thieving job starts at 10 gold. The buyer also pays a deposit, which covers jail fees and the replacement of anything stolen or damaged if the job goes wrong.
- **Who can't be targeted:** officially, the main bloodlines of the noble families.
- **If you've been robbed:** officially, you can't find out who hired them. Complaints are filed at the guild hall, a long process that is often settled in-house, though a high-profile case can go to the [[Fatebinders]].
- **The law:** members answer to the [[Astynomoi]].
- **Spotting a member:** officially, a black armband, though it's often not worn outside official settings.

## Notable Members
- Diodorus: leader of the guild. A short but lithe man, very basic-looking, with dark eyes and glasses, who dresses like an average commoner.
