*The officially sanctioned assassins of Hjirolis*

## Overview
The Moraktonoi are the sanctioned assassins of [[Hjirolis]]. A member is called a Moraktonos. Killings are carried out by legal writ, which keeps feuds between rival noble houses under the law's control.

They mirror the [[Kleptonomoi]], the sanctioned thieves, and are rivals with them.

## Hiring the Guild
- **Where:** the first guild hall going up the city is in Thema Tria.
- **Who can hire them:** anyone with the right gold, and the right paperwork. The paperwork is called a writ.
- **Cost:** very expensive. A basic kill on an unimportant target starts at 100 gold.
- **Who can't be targeted:** officially, the main bloodlines of the noble families.
- **If it goes wrong:** it tends not to. If it does, the guild takes responsibility.
- **If someone is killed:** a victim's family can almost never find out who hired them, but with enough digging they can find if its a legal or illegal killing. The Moraktonoi are very secretive.
- **Complaints:** filed at the guild hall, a long process that is often settled in-house, though a high-profile case can go to the [[Fatebinders]].
- **The law:** members don't answer to the [[Astynomoi]].
- **Spotting a member:** they have no official markings. Nobody knows what their leader looks like.
