*The grand ancient people whose ruins Hjirolis is built on*

## Overview
A grand ancient people, whose God-Emperor was Apolus, the [[Divine of Order]]. [[Hjirolis]] is built on their ruins, including the canals and the main government building of [[Ankarnassas]], and [[Ceropolis]] still reveres them. Almost nobody knows them as anything but legend and ruins.

## What the Party Knows
- [[Othenios Vyrik]] and the city of Ceropolis tell it this way: the Chain of Accord, a binding pact between tribes, ultimately led to their fall, and that failure was humanity's, not the Divine's ([[Session 7 Recap|Session 7]]).
- [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] tells it differently: they lived in harmony with the [[Shimmerwyld]], until gnomes following the Divine of Order tried to tame the wilds. The Isiliosi were nearly wiped out, and vanished into the forest ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]).
- They are thought to be linked to the ruins in the Shimmerwyld, such as [[Almurbalarammi]] and Nchuleft, and to the bastions, such as [[Bastion XIII]], though the party has its doubts about that last one ([[Session 22 Recap|Session 22]]).
