## Overview
The Sjuvven are one of the two human peoples of [[Hjirolis]]. They are taller, paler and lankier than the olive-skinned, broad-shouldered Hjirrans. For most of Hjir's history they were its lawful underclass of servants and labourers.

Hjirran law held them down in every way it could:
- A Sjuvven could not own land.
- A Sjuvven could not possess more than 10 gold.
- Sjuvven history could not be taught in schools.

These laws rested on a belief that the Sjuvven are inferior by blood. Depending on who tells it, an ancestor made a deal with a devil, or the Divines cursed their line. Many Hjirrans truly believed the Sjuvven were destined to cause great evil if they ever gained power, and plenty still do.

## The Nation of Sjuvven
Roughly 40 years ago, the Sjuvven rebelled and formed their own nation, also called Sjuvven, in the inhospitable edge of the wilds just northeast of Hjirolis. The chaos that followed was eventually calmed by the father of [[Autokrator Uluros IX]]. Hjir has had to work harder to make up for the lost labour ever since, and public sentiment for reconquering the Sjuvven is growing (see [[Hjir and Sjuvven Tensions]]).

## The Sjuvven of Hjirolis
Many Sjuvven stayed behind. Over the decades they have slowly built themselves up as merchants and tradesmen, though many are still poorly paid labourers and farmers. Most live in majority-Sjuvven communities in slum-like conditions, though some have made those neighbourhoods better.

In [[Ankarnassas]], most Sjuvven live in Iasipi (the Muds) in Thema Tessra and in the Wicks in Thema Pende.

Sjuvven culture puts hard work and self-reliance first. The hostility runs mostly one way, from Hjirrans toward Sjuvven. Most Sjuvven simply want equal opportunity, but as the city pushes them further out, frustration is growing.
