*The local militia and law enforcement of Hjirolis*

## Overview
The Astynomoi, often shortened to Asty, are the local militia and law enforcement of [[Hjirolis]]. They enforce the law, and the [[Fatebinders]] judge those they bring in.

They wear white togas over white-painted leather armour, and look more like monks than the armour-plated guards of other cities. Most carry staves, spears or both, as they prefer. They change shifts every six hours.

## What the Party Knows
- They're the guards the party has seen on the streets of [[Ankarnassas]].
- In Thema Pende and Shade Valley they keep to groups of at least six, led by a squad leader, and stop people for petty reasons.
- In the upper tiers they often appear in pairs and rarely interfere. In Ankar and Elikos they wear no armour at all.
