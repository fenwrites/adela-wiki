*Witch-like practicioners of spirit arts*


A [[Vedma]] is a witch/warlock. 

## What the Party Knows

- [[Nettalytyn Nyx]] called Mona a Vedma in training.
- At the [[Orreni Family|Orreni]] estate library, [[Mona]] learned that the term Vedma is associated with certain spirit-based magical practitioners in [[Ors]], on the east coast.
