*Strange, wilful beings of the natural world, invisible to most people*

## What They Are
Nobody the party has met can say for certain. Spirits are strange, wilful beings of the natural world, invisible to most people, and far more common in wild places than in towns. Stories about them grow rarer as civilization expands, and in the cities of [[Hjirolis]], [[Mona]] has found fewer than ever. [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]'s studies suggest why: Divines and their holy places are almost toxic to spirits, slowly draining their energy, and where there are many people, there are many worshippers.

## Those Who See Them
- [[Mona]] has seen and spoken with spirits all her life, and shapes them into magic through focused emotion.
- The [[Inseore Gnomes]] see them too, and have named and studied them. [[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]] built a wooden sensor that rattles when they're near, though he insists it doesn't work.
- [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] called himself and Mona [[Vedma]], a kind of spirit practitioner.
- [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]], strangely, has no spirits about her at all, as Nettle pointed out, despite growing up in the [[Shimmerwyld]] ([[Session 14 Recap|Session 14]]).

## Kinds of Spirit
- **Koro:** shy, reclusive and mushroom-like. They make tiny tools and pottery, and they love cheese, so a gift of it may draw them out. [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] told Mona about them.
- **Brùnaidh (brownies):** tiny, goblin-like and mischievous. [[Klau]] met one, Stealy Git, at Bert's Books in [[Wardensport]].

## Spirits and the Divines
Nettle told the party that the [[List of Divines|Divines]] and spirits stand opposed. He suggested that if they want to save their families, they should stop the Divines from entering the Shimmerwyld. He also marvelled at [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]], who holds both Divine power and the power of [[Shimmer]].

## What the Party Has Seen
- [[Mona]] saw spirits change as they left the [[Shimmerwyld]].
- On a [[Void Night]], the [[Green Gemstone|green gemstone]] glowed and spirits gathered around it ([[Session 15 Recap|Session 15]]).
- At [[Bastion XIII]], the harpy leader was performing a ritual of spirits and sacrifice ([[Session 22 Recap|Session 22]]).
