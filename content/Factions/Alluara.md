*The water gnomes, who split from the Inseore to keep their inventions*

The Alluara are gnomes who split from the [[Inseore Gnomes|Inseore]] long ago, refusing to give up their technologies when the [[Shimmerwyld]] turned on their ancestors. They live in caves beneath the water along the southern coast, and are said to be quite attractive.
