*Dissidents of outer Ankarnassas fighting for the poor*

## Overview
The Redwoods, named for [[Big Red]] and the Wicks, are a group of dissidents in outer [[Ankarnassas]]. They fight for equality, an end to persecution of the poor, and better living conditions outside the walls. Around the Wicks they are known for handing out free food at a few taverns and for funding the Temple of Growth's charity.

## Notable Members
- [[Big Red]]: ringleader.
