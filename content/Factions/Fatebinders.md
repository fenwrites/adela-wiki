## Overview
The judges of [[Hjirolis]]. Law enforcement falls to the [[Astynomoi]].

## Activities and Rumours
- After the party killed Ezolio in [[Loxos]], a Fatebinder released them, but warned them not to abuse their diplomatic power. It was already too late: their conduct cost them their posts as Saers' representatives.
- Kaynon Oriados, a senior Fatebinder in [[Ankarnassas]], is said to have a level head.
