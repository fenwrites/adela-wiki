## Overview
Gentle folk who live in harmony with the [[Shimmerwyld]]. Nature magic is common among them (druids, sorcerers, a cleric of Arshanta, an artificer or two), alongside hunters and rangers, but they are not fighters. Long ago the people of [[Hjirolis]] hated the gnomes. Relations are calmer now, if not exactly friendly.

## Customs
- Gnome society is matriarchal: when a couple marries, the man moves to his wife's glade.

## History
Their ancestors followed the [[Divine of Order]] into the Shimmerwyld and tried to tame it, until the forest turned on them. Those who refused to give up their technology became the Alluara, water gnomes who live in caves beneath the southern coast (and are said to be quite attractive). The rest kept the name Inseore as a reminder of their shame.

## Recent Events
- After infernal activity and the blight reached their glades, the Glimmerglade and Sunglade (84 gnomes in all) left the Shimmerwyld for Hjirolis, the first time in centuries.
