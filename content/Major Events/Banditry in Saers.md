*Food disruptions resulting from the blight have caused some to turn to banditry*

The food shortages caused by the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] have driven more and more people in [[Saers]] onto the roads. Many of them are farmers with nothing left. [[Audric Malikan]] has led crews into the hills near [[Primon Boundary]] to clear them out.

The party has run into them twice:
- **On the way to Pollard Farms** ([[Session 4 Recap|Session 4]]): bandits raiding a farmhouse claimed they had to steal or starve. One carried a note signed "AP".
- **On the road to [[Wardensport]]** ([[Session 6 Recap|Session 6]]): bandits posing as guards demanded a toll of a gold each.
