*Piece of a god that brought nature to Adela*

A piece of a god, which brought nature to [[Adela]]. [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] told the party the story ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]):

Before any nation, Adela was a sterile desert of magma fields, warmed by day by the star [[Lasaea]], with no stars at night. [[Arshanta (Being)|Arshanta]] watched, frustrated, until a piece of her crashed into Adela in a flash of white light. From it sprouted the World Tree, at what is now [[Arshanta's Grove]]. The gnomes call the forest that grew around it *Dae Taure Inse*, "Grove of the Fallen Star".
