*Fiends and other creatures from the hells appear to be trying to gain access to the shimmerwyld*

Fiends seem to be working their way into the [[Shimmerwyld]]. What [[The Party|the party]] has seen so far:

- **The cave** ([[Session 3 Recap|Session 3]]): as children, the party stumbled into a cave at the forest's edge, where [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] read an infernal note and a devil attacked them.
- **The portal** ([[Session 9 Recap|Session 9]]): deep in the eastern Shimmerwyld, a cave marked with the symbol of the [[Divine of Order]] held a planar portal to the infernal realm.
- **Almurbalarammi** ([[Session 12 Recap|Session 12]]): cultists held the ruin of [[Almurbalarammi]], and the party found a dead devil there, its body bearing a Z symbol.
- **The glades** ([[Session 14 Recap|Session 14]]): [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] warned that cultists had destroyed the Mossyglade, and the [[Glimmerglade]] and [[Sunglade]] left the forest under the party's escort.
- **Nchuleft** ([[Session 16 Recap|Session 16]]): the party rescued [[Tonks and Tansi]] and their children from cultists in another ruin.
