*The once subjugated Sjuvven people gained their independence from Hjir, but decades later tensions remain*

## Overview
Roughly 40 years ago, the [[Sjuvven]], the legal underclass of [[Hjirolis]], rebelled and founded their own nation in the wilds northeast of Hjir. The father of [[Autokrator Uluros IX]] restored order, but the wound never closed.

- **In Hjir proper**, everyone has had to work harder to make up for the lost Sjuvven labour, and resentment has built up. More and more people say the Sjuvven nation should be conquered and its people subjugated again.
- **A failed assassination attempt** on the Autokrator has made war look inevitable.
- **The Sjuvven who stayed** in Hjir face ongoing prejudice and live mostly in poor, majority-Sjuvven neighbourhoods.
- **The [[Divine of Order]]**, Apolus, appeared during violent debates in the agora over war with the Sjuvven, decreeing that *"those that seek to foster Chaos have no right within Hjirolis."* Each side has read that its own way.

## In Ankarnassas
Tension in the capital is rising. A rebel known as [[Big Red]] is said to be stoking the flames and fighting for Sjuvven equality ([[Session 23 Recap|Session 23]]).
