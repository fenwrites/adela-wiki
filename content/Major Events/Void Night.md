*The starless night that divides the ten months of the year*

## Overview
Across [[Ucaro and Nosovae]], most people keep a calendar of ten months, divided by one-day periods known as **Voids**, also called Darklight, Spirit's Hallow and many other names. For a single night, every star in the sky vanishes. Understandably, this has bred many superstitions. There are plenty of theories about why it happens, but no definitive answer.
