*A strange blight destroying crops and infecting people emerging from the Shimmerwyld*

## What It Is
A blight that destroys crops and infects people, spreading out from around the [[Shimmerwyld]]. Its food shortages have driven people to [[Banditry in Saers|banditry]] and turned public sentiment in [[Saers]] against the forest. [[Orlean Vetiver]] pushed for a War Mantle to clear it, and [[Audric Malikan]] has resisted.

## How It Works
The sick begin with a cough, then suffer nightmares and exhaustion, then necrosis and the growth of spores. Those who die rise as zombies riddled with it, which burst into clouds of spores when destroyed ([[Session 5 Recap|Session 5]]). Caught early, strong healing magic can cure it: [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] cured himself and [[Mona]] ([[Session 11 Recap|Session 11]]).

It reaches animals too. The party has killed a blighted vorebeast and a blighted owlbear, and cured the blight from a root shrine to Arshanta ([[Session 10 Recap|Sessions 10]] and [[Session 15 Recap|15]]).

## Where It Came From
Nobody knows for sure.
- [[Waylan Wiles]] believes chaos magic is rising from one direction, toward the old cultist fort, and suspected Pollard Farms outside [[Tollen Boundary]]. There the party found Blighted zombies, forbidden seed storage and signs of cult activity ([[Session 4 Recap|Session 4]]).
- [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] writes that Velesara's clerics can only hold it at bay, because it doesn't fit their worldview: it is chaotic, a living thing that keeps growing.
- [[Augustus Fearly]] leads the research for Saers.

## Who It Has Hurt
- [[Samira]], Delrek and Klau's little sister, is very sick. Other children and elderly people living near the forest have died.
- Tollen Boundary has been deserted, and [[Primon Boundary]] has emptied out, leaving only priests and soldiers.
- It has also reached the [[Glimmerglade]], and helped drive the [[Inseore Gnomes]] from their homes.

## A Possible Lead
[[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]] and [[Vivica Glimmerglade|Vivica]] suggested that Glasspetal, a rare clear flower that feeds on toxins in swamp caves, might help against it ([[Session 14 Recap|Session 14]]).
