*The twelve star signs of Adela, what they mean, and what they grant*

The sign you're born under shapes you for life, and each sign is **present** for part of each year (see [[Calendar]]). While your own sign is present, you are Empowered.

## The Party
- [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]]: the Empress
- [[Klau]]: [[Shalar]]
- [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]: the Chameleon
- [[Mona]]: the Faerie
- [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]: the Faerie

## How to Read a Sign
- **Boon / Penalty:** the ability score the sign favours, and the one it costs you.
- **Proficiency:** the skill you gain.
- **Fate dice:** the size of your fate dice pool. You can spend a fate die to reroll any d20 once.

## The Signs

### The Soldier
*Present from Morning Star 28.* The Soldier represents protection and duty, and the power of the common folk. Those born under it tend to be hard-working members of society, blessed with strength, but promised violence in their lifetimes.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Dex / Int. **Proficiency:** Athletics. **Fate dice:** 3.
- **Dauntless:** once per long rest, when you gain a point of exhaustion, roll a d6. On a 2+, ignore it and gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus plus the die.
- **Empowered:** a supernatural force guides your strikes, and you gain double your proficiency bonus to hit.

### The Empress
*Present from First Seed 28.* The Empress represents the leadership and power of people as a whole, and their ability to overcome the world around them. Those born under it tend to be individualistic and driven.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Cha / Con. **Proficiency:** Persuasion. **Fate dice:** 4.
- **Visionary:** gain the Inspiring Leader feat.
- **Empowered:** when you spend a fate die, roll a d6. On a 2+, you get the die back.

### The Beast
*Present from Rain's Peak 27.* The Beast represents the bond between people and the wild. Animals gravitate to those born under it, and they tend to fight best beside others, with an instinct for the pack.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Str / Wis. **Proficiency:** Animal Handling. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Pack Leader:** flanking bonuses only require an ally adjacent to the enemy. Once per turn, an attack made with advantage from flanking can reroll its damage dice and use the new value.
- **Empowered:** you can cast *animal friendship* once per day, with Strength as your spellcasting modifier, and the target makes its save with disadvantage.

### The Divine
*Present from Lanathas 23.* The Divine represents the embodiment of a singular trait to the extreme. Those born under it often feel the stars whispering to them, and seem to be guided out of harm's way.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Wis / Dex. **Proficiency:** Religion. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Starlight Seer:** once per long rest, use your reaction to gain advantage on a saving throw.
- **Empowered:** your Starlight Seer automatically passes the saving throw.

### The Wayfarer
*Present from Second Seed 19.* Also called the Path. Those rare few born under it are hard to keep in one place, and are considered destined for greatness, but also for an uphill battle.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Wis / Cha. **Proficiency:** Survival. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **I Walk the Path:** once per day, cast *expeditious retreat* without using a spell slot.
- **Empowered:** you gain an additional 5 ft of movement.

### The Dragon
*Present from Sunbright 20.* Those born under the Dragon are often free-spirited and lucky, the children who listened intently to the old stories of heroes and dragons when others cowered.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Str / Int. **Proficiency:** History. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Dragonheart:** advantage on saving throws against fear, and on History checks to recall old legends you've heard.
- **Empowered:** at the start of each day, channel a legend of a famous dragon you've heard of, and gain resistance to its damage type.

### The Chameleon
*Present from Hamnor 20.* Those born under the Chameleon are unpredictable, often troublesome children with the potential for greatness under a steady hand. They pick up on their surroundings quickly, and fit in anywhere.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Dex / Con. **Proficiency:** Stealth. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Code Switching:** spend 1d6+1 hours picking up the local customs of an area. You then gain +2 to all Charisma checks in that region, and advantage on Charisma-based checks when impersonating locals.
- **Empowered:** you can cast *invisibility* once per day for free.

### The Mountain
*Present from Frostfall 3.* Those born under the Mountain are resilient, having had time to grow before the harsh winter hits. Even as children, they are hard to push around.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Con / Dex. **Proficiency:** Nature. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Immutable:** advantage on checks and saves against being knocked prone. If an effect moves you against your will along the ground, use your reaction to reduce the distance by up to 10 ft.
- **Empowered:** you can't be knocked prone or moved against your will, and you have advantage on Constitution saving throws.

### Shalar
*Present from Winterstar 1, when the distant blue star [[Shalar]] bathes the world in its light.* Those born under it are considered incredibly lucky and magical, inherently linked to the Aether. Others exclaim over them as children, when their eyes glow bright and they see the thin layer of magic coating the world.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Int / Str. **Proficiency:** Arcana. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Aetherborne:** once per day, cast *detect magic* for free.
- **Empowered:** you gain the *ray of frost* cantrip, using Intelligence. If you already know it, it does an additional 1d8 damage.

### The Faerie
*Present from Diminus 1.* Those born under the Faerie have always noticed oddities that others can't, and were often told they were just seeing things. They are hard to fool, and harder to frighten.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Cha / Str. **Proficiency:** Insight. **Fate dice:** 3.
- **Extrasensory Perception:** you can attempt to identify spells being cast in combat for free, and investigate illusions as a bonus action. Fey are always immediately identifiable to you.
- **Empowered:** advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened. If you already have advantage, you are immune.

### The River
*Present from Diminus 37, closing out the year.* The River represents the pattern within nature's chaos. Those born under it read the land easily, and always seem to find the way through it.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Con / Cha. **Proficiency:** Nature. **Fate dice:** 2.
- **Nature's Pattern:** once per day during a short rest, study the nature within 10 km. You learn whether the landscape has been unnaturally altered or disturbed recently, what wildlife is present and where it gathers, and the quickest path through. At the end of a short rest, regain 1d4 hit points per hour rested. Repeat this in one region every day for a year, and you become magically connected to it, sensing the movement of living things within.
- **Empowered:** you ignore difficult terrain, and Nature's Pattern healing increases to a d8.

### The Void
*Present on each [[Void Night]], when the stars vanish.* Those born when the sky is empty are seen as cursed and unlucky, and are often mourned from birth, though in more developed places many go on to live normal, successful lives. To them, the dark has always felt natural.
- **Boon / Penalty:** Int / Wis. **Proficiency:** Arcana. **Fate dice:** 1.
- **Voideyes:** gain 60 ft of darkvision. As an action, focus on magical darkness to see through it as if it wasn't there.
- **Empowered:** all Wisdom saves are made with disadvantage that can't be cancelled by advantage, but all other saves are made with advantage.
