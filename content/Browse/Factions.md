## Factions & Peoples
- [[Spirits]] — Strange, wilful beings of the natural world, invisible to most people
- [[Alluara Gnomes]] — The water gnomes, who split from the Inseore to keep their inventions
- [[Astynomoi]] — The local militia and law enforcement of Hjirolis
- [[Brussaer]] — Forefront of arcane studies in Saers
- [[Dawn of the Midnight Sun]] — Cultist group with inscrutable goals
- [[Fatebinders]]
- [[Inseore Gnomes]] — Once-invaders of the Shimmerwyld turned nature-loving dwellers of the trees
- [[Isiliosi]] — The grand ancient people whose ruins Hjirolis is built on
- [[Klau's Quills]] — The party's own company, with Klau's name on it as a joke and Tharr's paperwork behind it
- [[Kleptonomoi]] — The officially sanctioned thieves of Ankarnassas, a frowned-upon part of doing business
- [[Moraktonoi]] — The officially sanctioned assassins of Hjirolis
- [[Naltése Peoples]] — Ancient group of bugbears that have lived on islands and coastlines for all of history
- [[Redwoods]] — Dissidents of outer Ankarnassas fighting for the poor
- [[Saers Mercantile Guild]] — Merchants guild of saers
- [[Seanchai Guild]] — Storytellers guild of saers
- [[Sjuvven]] — Tall, pale people once held as Hjirolis's legal underclass, now split between their own northern nation and the slums of Hjir
- [[Vedma]] — Witch-like practicioners of spirit arts
