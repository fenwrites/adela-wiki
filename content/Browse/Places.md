## The World
- [[Adela]] — The entire world

- [[Ucaro]] — The southern landmass twin of Nosovae, one half of the continent
- [[Ucaro and Nosovae]] — The continent formed by the twin landmasses of Ucaro and Nosovae

## Nations
- [[Hjirolis]] — Opportunistic order loving civilization of humans
- [[Miskral]] — Homeland of halflings that seems to survive on charisma
- [[Ors]] — Underestimated nation of goblinoids who have pioneered artificing and unique art forms
- [[Saers]] — Divine-led peaceful expansionist nation that promotes community above all else
- [[The Seven Cities]] — A conglomerate of powerful city-states whose highest power is currency
- [[Xegora]] — Colonist elves that have become hermits in their mysterious mountain range

## Towns of Saers
- [[Primon Boundary]] — A frontier town along a river delta that has existed peacefully for decades
- [[Saers (City)]] — The capitol of Saers, a center for commerce, diversity, culture, and civilization

## Glades of the Shimmerwyld
- [[Glimmerglade]] — A quiet and peaceful gnome grove
- [[Stonyglade]] — A gnome grove nestled in the northern mountain range within the Shimmerwyld
- [[Sunglade]] — A gnome grove built high up in the trees

## Landmarks
- [[Arshanta's Grove]] — Mythical center of the Shimmerwyld that is seemingly impossible to find
- [[Leukotichos]] — A mountain range in northern Hjirolis, just north of Ankarnassas
- [[Orronoké]] — A supervolcano and important cultural homeland of the Naltese peoples
- [[Shimmerwyld]] — A massive magical forest filled with all manner of creatures and spirits

## Cities & Regions
- [[Almurbalarammi]] — Ancient ruin of 'silence' and site of a fight between cultists and the party
- [[Ankarnassas]] — Capitol of Hjir nestled in mountain foothills and built on the lakeside ruins of ancient civilization
- [[Ceropolis]] — Thriving port-adjacent center of agriculture and trade in Hjir
- [[Hasitoppe]] — Ancient and obstinate civilization of the dwarves
- [[Oranos]] — An agricultural community that sees political deals due to its proximity to Ankarnassas
- [[Pelethra]] — A small port-city that acts as access to ceropolis and the Hjirolis interior
- [[Tollen Boundary]] — A north-eastern frontier town beset upon by cultists
- [[Wardensport]] — A port-city of saers
