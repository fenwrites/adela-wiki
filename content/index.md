## The Story So Far…
*Five adventurers of growing renown have arrived in the capital of [[Hjirolis]], the sprawling tiered city of [[Ankarnassas]], nestled in the [[Leukotichos]] mountains.*

**From [[Primon Boundary]]:** a trio of youths, [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]], [[Klau]], and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]], bound by origin and destiny. After stumbling into the [[Shimmerwyld]] during a trial for adulthood, the three became wrapped up in a plot far grander than they could ever imagine. Years later they returned, discovered a growing cult, and were employed by [[Saers]] to investigate the far side of the Shimmerwyld, in Hjirolis. 

Upon arrival in [[Ceropolis]], a port city in southern Hjir, they met the local [[Aoran Aurelius|Archon]], whom their travelling companion and envoy of Saers, [[Fibbart Miller]] came to meet with. Prior to the meeting, a serendipitous occurrence. A young woman, [[Mona]], fell from the ceiling under mysterious circumstances. She was able to quickly buy the party's (at least temporary) trust by telling them the directions to meet with the mysterious [[Inseore Gnomes]] that lived in the Shimmerwyld before vanishing.

In the fringe of the Shimmerwyld, the party was beset upon by a swarm of dangerous razor-teethed mushroom creatures, but saved by [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]], a local Inseore. Wary of the party, she agreed to travel with them after the group stumbled upon a cavern that was clearly associated with infernals. They rushed to tell the gnomes living nearby, regrouping with Mona, but infernal events were already in action.

The group prioritized rescuing the gnomes, who they escorted out of the Shimmerwyld. While the gnomes were experts of their domain, they knew little of the infernal threat and Hjirran politics, so the party put them in the hands of the [[Orreni Family]], a relatively trustworthy noble family whose estates were nearby. 

At the same time, the party heard the news spreading rapidly through the lands: The Divine of Order has manifested in the flesh as [[Divine of Order|Apolus]], come to lead the nation out of chaos.

Seeking to give their information to an informant so they might help their families back home in Saers, and with a strange [[Green Gemstone|green gemstone]] with some living energy inside, the party travelled north through [[Oranos]]. There, they stopped and parlayed with [[Gyrobastille]], a Saers spy, who informed them of rapidly changing laws within Hjirolis. Here, a strange book carried by Klau, [[The Chronicle]], attracted the attention of a powerful Harpy, who nearly severed his soul. He was saved by the book itself, pulled into a strange place, where [[The Saracens|people]] told Klau destructive [[List of Divines|Divines]] would be returning to [[Adela]] within 7 years.

Despite losing their role as official representatives of [[Saers]] (due to incompetence) and this foreboding warning, the party continued on to [[Ankarnassas]] seeking answers.
