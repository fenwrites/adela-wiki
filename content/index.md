## Previously…
*Five adventurers of growing renown have arrived in the capital of [[Hjirolis]], the sprawling walled city of [[Ankarnassas]].*

**From [[Primon Boundary]]:** a trio of youths, bound by origin and destiny. After stumbling into the [[Shimmerwyld]] during a trial for adulthood, the three became wrapped up in a plot far grander than they could ever imagine. Years later they returned, discovered a growing cult, and were employed by [[Saers]] to investigate the far side of the Shimmerwyld, in Hjirolis. While competent in battle, heroes to the [[Inseore Gnomes]] they rescued from an apparent devilish invasion, and with valuable information learned, their repeated political flubs left them jobless. They continued on to Ankarnassas for a plethora of reasons.
