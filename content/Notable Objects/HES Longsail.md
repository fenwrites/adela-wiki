*The sleek cog that carried the party from Saers to Hjirolis*

## Summary
A sleek cog in Her Empress's Service, with a long single sail that its captain proudly helped design. It carried the party roughly 3,000 km across the [[Southmoon Sea]], from [[Wardensport]] to [[Pelethra]] ([[Session 7 Recap|Session 7]]).

## Crew and Passengers
- [[Azure “Hop”|Captain Hop]]
- [[Argus Cooky|Argus "Cooky"]], the ship's cook
- [[Lady Aleera Valeteene]], travelling to her marriage
- [[Tyna Smallfoot]], a halfling messenger
- [[Quintus Maximus]], an envoy from [[Hjirolis]]
- [[The Party]], with [[Fibbart Miller]] and [[Garnet Teimster]]
