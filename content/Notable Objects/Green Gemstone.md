*A pale green gem with a voice inside, which Illy keeps alive with her own magic*

## Summary
A pale green gem with a voice inside, which [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] keeps alive with her own magic. Nobody the party has asked knows what it is.

## What the Party Knows
- The party recovered it at [[Almurbalarammi]] ([[Session 12 Recap|Session 12]]). When Illy touched it, a voice asked her: *"Help me."*
- Another green gem turned up in the same ruin, smashed, inside the corpse of a devil whose body bore a Z symbol.
- [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] and [[Agatha Sunglade|Agatha]] couldn't identify it, and Agatha suggested seeking counsel in [[Ankarnassas]] ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]).
- It has already caused friction in the party, passing from Illy to [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] to [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] and back ([[Session 15 Recap|Session 15]]).
- [[Gyrobastille|Gyro]] suggested [[Flavius Tellus]], a gemstone magic expert at the Lyceum of Alcion.
