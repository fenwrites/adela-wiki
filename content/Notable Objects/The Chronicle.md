*Magical book for two-way communication*

[[Klau]]

[[The Chronicle]] is a thick tome, but not unwieldy. It has a nice weight to it and appears to be in pristine condition. It's bound in a dark leather that is thin but strong, and while you might not be a tanner (haha), you would expect with your experience with clothing and materials you would be able to identify it–yet you cannot.

  

The pages are inconsistently cut, the variations revealing a pattern upon closer inspection. First, there are twenty pages with clean, precise edges, followed by twelve pages that are cut more roughly, almost jarringly so. These transitions gradually give way over the next thirty or so pages to a more refined finish, though not as immaculate as the initial section. This sequence repeats three times throughout the book, totalling 186 pages.

  

The entire book is blank, except for the first page where “[[Klau]] set sail into a new adventure” is written in cursive. The handwriting is reminiscent of a scholar; someone who has mastered the art of calligraphy (in contrast to the writing you often you read of storytellers, who tend to be more concerned with what's on the parchment than with how it's on the parchment).


Written At Some Point:

Gooday, my name is [[Klau]] Starlight, member of the Keepers of Past & Present, lover of history, teller of tales. My dear book, I was wondering if you could tell me more about yourself? Do you have a name other than '[[The Chronicle]]', are you the book itself or something more omniscient? If you would enjoy a conversation then I am a willing ear. Come and know me better.
[[Klau]] set sail into a new adventure



Contents of [[the Chronicle]]

Chronicle Keeper: [[Klau]] Starlight

Our story begins 1,200 years ago...Cormac of the Village (the village is not named, basically to show that the dragonborn were nomads and settlements did not last long)...Cormac wished to unite the Dragonborn under one banner...Cormac believed himself to the descendent of the great dragon, Malikan the Magnificent...He climbed to the highest peak of the Jagged Talon Range which took him two weeks...Malikan was amused at the small creature so he set him a set of chalenges..."Move my claw and show me your strength" He flicked off the top of a stalagmite and buried his claw in the stone.

In [[the Chronicle]], there are two footnotes on '1,200 years ago' (1,189), and on 'two weeks' ('Only 6 days and nights actually, but a fortnight does sound a lot better').
