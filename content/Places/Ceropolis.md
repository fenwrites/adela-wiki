#town 

[[Hjirolis]]

## Overview
A regional capital of [[Hjirolis]] on the opposite side of the country from [[Ankarnassas]], ruled by Archon [[Aoran Aurelius]]. Ships dock at the nearby port of [[Pelethra]]. The city is built among ancient ruins, and for a city that worships Order, it is strikingly disorderly: ships sit unloaded and work backs up, as labour has grown scarce since the Sjuvven gained their freedom.

## Notable Features
- The gate of Apolus, a massive marble gate under construction.
- Tarmios Square, the mercantile centre, where orators call to march north or to re-enslave the Sjuvven.
- The Acropolis, with the Archon's residence, a bathhouse, the Akademos Lykeion university, and a throne room whose stained glass shows Apolus and the Great Binding.
- The Tanked Trireme (inn and tavern by the harbour), Thaumagora (a magic shop in an ancient ruin, run by Kyra Heliodora), Pyraios Foundry (Karnos Sophos), a Sjuvven apothecary (Merric Austenio), the bookshop Bindings of Eternity, and the Amphitheater of Lyceus.
- New Dawn, the Sjuvven district to the north.
- Arena fights, where Tharr fought under the name "Bugbear".
