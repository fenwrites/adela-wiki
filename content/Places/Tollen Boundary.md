*A north-eastern frontier town beset upon by cultists*

#town

[[Saers]]

## Overview
A frontier town smaller than [[Primon Boundary]], quiet and moody since the blight. Pollard Farms lies on its outskirts.

## Activities and Rumours
- Ralof fled the town carrying his son, raving that ordinary folk "with smiles" had tried to kill them.
- Three townsfolk ambushed the party at night. Each had a three-inch metal control spike driven into the base of the skull.
- By the time of [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]]'s family's letter, the town had been deserted after many fell sick.
