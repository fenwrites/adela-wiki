*The continent formed by the twin landmasses of Ucaro and Nosovae*

The great continent of [[Adela]] is made up of two landmasses: [[Nosovae]] to the north and [[Ucaro]] to the south.

![[Ucaro and Nosovae.png]]

## Overview
The continent has been relatively peaceful for about three centuries, with only small conflicts rather than empire-shattering wars. The long peace has brought growing populations, increasingly complex politics, territorial expansion, and more trade and travel between the two landmasses.

There are roughly ten large nations, several smaller ones, a dozen or so independent cities and hundreds of settlements. Their cultures range from xenophobic to multicultural, from Divine-led to nearly atheist, and from sprawling cities to tribal bands and slave-holding merchant empires.
