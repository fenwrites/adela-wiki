*The Great Red, a lethal holy desert of iron sand in central Hasitoppe*


## Overview
Also called **The Great Red**: a vast desert in central [[Hasitoppe]], long revered as a holy and magical place. Its blood-red sand is rich in iron, heavy and coarse. Extreme winds raise sandstorms thousands of feet high, with wild displays of lightning as electricity leaps between the flying metal. It is extremely lethal, and a few pilgrims die every year walking the holy trail around it. Some say there is an oasis at its centre, but nobody knows for sure.
