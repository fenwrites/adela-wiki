*Ancient ruin of 'silence' and site of a fight between cultists and the party*

[[Shimmerwyld]]

- An ancient ruin about two days northwest of the [[Glimmerglade]] that is made of a strange, dark brown metallic material, almost like bronze but with less shine. Growing up, [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] would have heard stoires about it, y'know ghost stories, stories about how people used to live there a long time ago, stories about a happy couple that called the place home, stories about ancient [[spirits]] that had built the place as a home. The vibe around it is that the place is super interesting from a historical perspective, but incredibly boring otherwise. Nothing of note has ever happened there, there is nothing inside but a few old pieces of furniture. Its just an entryway and a large chamber. Its dusty. So, for the most part, [[Inseore gnomes]] enjoy telling stories about it, but aren't actually all that interested.
- The ruin is dome-shaped from above, about 30 feet tall. Inside is a large entrychamber with ancient bedframes and a few tables, all grown directly into the floor. Wall sconces shine shadows across the ruin. The floor and walls are smooth and nondescript. 
- On the walls are a faded inscription that reads: "[[Almurbalarammi]]: A Fragile Creation, In One Day It's Name Breaks It" It continues but is indecipherable.

## Notes
- The inscription is written in Old Common.
- Nobody is sure who once lived here. One theory says the [[Naltese Peoples]] once lived in unison with the [[Shimmerwyld]], another that ancient humans thrived here.
