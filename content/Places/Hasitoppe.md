*Ancient and obstinate civilization of the dwarves*


[[Hasitoppe]] is an incredibly long-standing civilization of ancient dwarves with strict customs of minimal involvement with the outer world, but whose border has slowly been tempted to break those customs by the draw of trade/globalization.

Dwarves are the only ones allowed to own property. Dwarves are the only who can get married, and only to other dwarves. Dwarves from outside [[Hasitoppe]] are see as distasteful. An endless debates rages on about the nature of life: Is something only created in a moment, like an explosion, most beautiful, or are things that stand the test of time the most beautiful? Is the natural of reality one in which everything can be understood as a science, or is there true 'magic' beyond mortal conception?

## Overview
The longest-standing nation on the continent and the homeland of the dwarves, who rarely go abroad, preferring their enduring architecture carved from mountains and earth. The land is diverse and well defended, from coastal waters and plains to mountains and the great desert of [[Hasirothe]], "The Great Red", which dominates the centre of the nation and draws pilgrims to walk its holy trail.

Politics here is slow, with change coming in small increments. The [[Divine of Arts]] is Hasitoppe's main Divine, and is said to have appeared there in person.
