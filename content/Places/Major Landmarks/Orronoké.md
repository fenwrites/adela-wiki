*A supervolcano and important cultural homeland of the Naltése peoples*


- The [[Naltése Peoples|Naltése]] say the Great Beast lies asleep in the warmth of the volcano. The island is uninhabited, but explorers have found strange creatures unique to it. Trying to reach the volcano without the Naltése's blessing is a dangerous game.
