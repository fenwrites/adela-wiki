*A supervolcano and important cultural homeland of the Naltese peoples*


- The [[Naltese Peoples|Naltese]] say the Great Beast lies asleep in the warmth of the volcano. The island is uninhabited, but explorers have found strange creatures unique to it. Trying to reach the volcano without the Naltese's blessing is a dangerous game.
