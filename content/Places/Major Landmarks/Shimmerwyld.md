*A massive magical forest filled with all manner of creatures and spirits*

The Shimmerwyld is a vast, enchanted forest at the heart of [[Nosovae]], with [[Saers]] on its western side and [[Hjirolis]] on its eastern. Its canopy and undergrowth shimmer with an ever-present magical aura, which gives the forest its name. Centuries of expanding civilization have pushed back its borders, but it has never been tamed, and lately something in it seems to be going wrong.

## What Everyone Knows
- The forest is dangerous, though less so than in ancient times.
- Explorers often vanish, and only a few have ever crossed it entirely.
- It resists permanent settlement. Attempts to tame it tend to be short-lived, as nature takes the land back.
- Fugitives, cultists and outcasts have long used it as a refuge. [[Sorhan Malikan]] drove one such cult out before founding [[Primon Boundary]].
- Unique creatures live within its borders, some seen nowhere else in Nosovae.

## The Land
Towering ancient trees, bioluminescent fungi, crystalline rivers, steep cliffed valleys and sprawling glades make up the forest, all of it steeped in chaotic magic. Wildlife mutates quickly here, and sentient plants and magical beasts are part of the landscape. [[The Party|The party]] has fought swarms of razor-teethed mushroom creatures and a blighted vorebeast, and met a pair of quarrelling ents.

At its heart stands the World Tree, in the mythical and seemingly unreachable [[Arshanta's Grove]]. Common tales make it several hundred feet tall. In Delrek's vision, it was far larger.

## Who Lives Here
- **The [[Inseore Gnomes]]:** gentle folk who live in harmony with the forest and act as its guardians, in glades along the Hjirolis border. Many revere [[Arshanta (Being)|Arshanta]].
- **[[Shimmer]]:** a botanical being of ever-moving vines, whose power now grows inside Delrek.
- **[[Spirits]]:** the Inseore can see them, name them and study them, and [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] can tell you which ones love cheese.

## The Forest's Story
As Per [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]):

Before any nation, [[Adela]] was a sterile desert of magma fields. [[Arshanta (Being)|Arshanta]] watched, frustrated, until a piece of her crashed into the world in a flash of white light, and from it sprouted the World Tree ([[Arshanta's Comet]]). The gnomes call the forest that grew around it *Dae Taure Inse*, "Grove of the Fallen Star".

Much later, the [[Isiliosi]] lived here in harmony with the forest. Then gnomes following the [[Divine of Order]] arrived and tried to tame the wilds. The Isiliosi were nearly wiped out and vanished into the trees, and the woods themselves came alive and nearly destroyed the gnomes. The survivors split into the [[Alluara Gnomes|Alluara]], who kept their inventions, and the Inseore, who kept their name as a reminder of their shame.

[[Hjirolis]] tells the Isiliosi's fall differently. See [[Isiliosi]].

## Notable Places
- **The glades:** [[Glimmerglade]], [[Sunglade]], [[Stonyglade]], the Meadowglade and the Mossyglade.
- **[[Arshanta's Grove]]:** the World Tree and the forest's mythical centre.
- **[[Almurbalarammi]]:** an ancient domed ruin, northwest of the Glimmerglade. It's one of twelve ruins on a map the party found carved in another ruin, Nchuleft. Several lie outside the forest's current borders.
- **The Witchgrove:** a swamp northeast of the Sunglade with an old wooden house inside, and a possible home of Glasspetal flowers.

## What's Happening Now
- **The [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]]:** the blight is spreading out from around the forest, and has turned many in [[Saers]] against it. [[Orlean Vetiver]] has pushed to clear the forest, while the Malikan family has fought against logging and invasion. [[Waylan Wiles]] believes chaos magic in the forest is rising from one direction, possibly from an old cultist fort.
- **[[Infernal In the Shimmerwyld|Infernals]]:** the party has found a portal to the infernal realm, cultists holding ancient ruins and a dead devil marked with a Z.
- **The gnome exodus:** [[Nettalytyn Nyx|Nettle]] warned that cultists had destroyed the Mossyglade. The Glimmerglade and Sunglade left the forest under the party's escort, the first time in centuries, and are now working with the [[Orreni Family|Orreni]].
- **An unsettled forest:** [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] found the animals around the World Tree unusually aggressive on a recent trip. On a [[Void Night]], spirits gathered around the party's [[Green Gemstone|green gemstone]].
- **The Divines:** Nettle described an opposition between the [[List of Divines|Divines]] and the spirits, and told the party that if they wanted to save their families, they should stop the Divines from entering the forest.
- **Hjirolis:** preoccupied with its own affairs, and largely ignoring all of this.
- **A missing boy:** Iocles, the nephew of [[Cyphus Orreni]], disappeared in the forest. The party searched a witch hut, his last known location, and found no sign of him.

## Uncommon Trees
- **Glimmerwood Trees:** tall, silver-barked trees with leaves that shimmer faintly in moonlight, often used for enchanted woodcraft.
- **Umbral Pines:** dark-needled conifers that absorb light, making them ideal for stealth enchantments.
- **Verdant Wyrmbarks:** massive, gnarled trees with serpentine roots, believed to be infused with draconic magic.
- **Aurora Birches:** white-barked birches whose leaves glow with shifting colours at dusk.
- **Thornmire Willows:** willows with long, thorny vines, often used by druids for defensive spells.
- **Feyshade Oaks:** ancient oaks with purple-hued leaves, known to be favoured by fey creatures.
- **Ironbark Cedars:** trees with bark as tough as steel, often sought for armour and weapon crafting.
