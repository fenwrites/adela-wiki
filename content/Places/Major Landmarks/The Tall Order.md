*A rickety, creaking tavern stacked four wooden storeys high in Thema Pende*

Stone walls at street level, and on top of them four storeys of timber that nobody seems to have planned. The Tall Order is one of the cheapest places to sleep in Thema Pende, and it groans and creaks like it knows it. It doesn't look especially stable, but that has never hurt business. The ground floor is a crowded, well-loved tavern, run by [[Twillius Unc|Twunk]].

Something very large moves around in the kitchen. The party caught a glimpse of the figure, but no more than that ([[Session 24 Recap|Session 24]]).
