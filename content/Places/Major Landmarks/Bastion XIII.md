*A crumbling bastion outside Oranos, with a hidden dungeon beneath it*

## Overview
A crumbling stone tower and fort in Sael Oros, the bare hills outside [[Oranos]]. An engraved plaque lying in the rubble names it "Bastion XIII". A coven of harpies had made it their den, and it was here that their leader lured the bewitched [[Klau]] ([[Session 21 Recap]], [[Session 22 Recap]]).

## History
Several of these limestone bastions are scattered along the eastern [[Shimmerwyld]]. They are usually said to be Isiliosi watchtowers, built as an early warning against dangerous creatures emerging from the forest. That story has holes: this bastion stands within Sael Oros, which was *inside* the Shimmerwyld only a century ago, and scholars debate whether the bastions are Isiliosi at all, since their architecture differs from other Isiliosi ruins.

## The Dungeon
Beneath the fort is a dungeon entrance that someone had deliberately hidden, despite public archaeological efforts in the area. The party took a few steps inside, retreated, and hid the entrance again.
