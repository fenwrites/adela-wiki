Mountain range in northern [[Hjirolis]]
Just north of [[Ankarnassas]]

## Overview
These mountains are considered a wall between civilization and the wilds to the north. [[Ankarnassas]], the capital of [[Hjirolis]], sits at the foot of the first peaks.
