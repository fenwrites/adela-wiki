*A mountain range in northern Hjirolis, just north of Ankarnassas*

## Overview
A range of limestone peaks across northern [[Hjirolis]], considered a wall between civilization and the wilds to the north. [[Ankarnassas]], the capital, sits at the foot of the first peaks, and [[Oranos]] lies in the foothills.

## North of the Mountains
There has been harpy activity north of the range. It's also home to the Sinok, a druidic, chaotic people. The harpies the party fought at [[Bastion XIII]] used a ritual circle of Sinok runes ([[Session 22 Recap|Session 22]]).
