*The Ancient Bridge of Stars, spanning the Danar river in Aghadan*

## Overview
The Ancient Bridge of Stars, or Suranon Agal, spans the massive Danar river in Aghadan. It is said to have been built by ancient monks for an inscrutable purpose, and is protected by magic so that it never degrades. Scenes from the stars are carved and inlaid in precious metals and gems along its length, and a strange lens lets visitors magnify and view the heavens themselves.
