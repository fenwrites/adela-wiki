*Mythical center of the Shimmerwyld that is seemingly impossible to find*

The mythical centre of the [[Shimmerwyld]], and home of the World Tree of [[Arshanta (Being)|Arshanta]]. It's seemingly impossible to find, though [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] says she travelled there recently, and found the animals unusually aggressive ([[Session 13 Recap|Session 13]]).

[[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] saw it in a vision: a massive tree of unfathomable height, with translucent leaves of every colour, rising from a gorge over a thousand metres deep. Several whole biomes seemed to thrive beneath its canopy, and massive flying creatures wheeled in the distance.
