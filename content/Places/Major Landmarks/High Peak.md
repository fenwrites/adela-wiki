*A massive mountain range on Ucaro*


## Overview
A massive mountain range dominating southeastern [[Ucaro]], visible from great distances. It is a refuge for things tough enough to survive there, such as giants and dragons, and strange, powerful effects prevent magical travel in the region, so even the greatest wizards can't claim to have conquered its peak.
