*Underestimated nation of goblinoids who have pioneered artificing and unique art forms*

Feral Reach Instability

## Overview
**Peoples:** mostly orcs, goblins and bugbears.

A nation of orcs who left the barbaric life of the Feral Reach and banded together to escape their violent history. It took decades of war with [[Hjirolis]] and the coastal [[Naltese Peoples|Naltese]], but they eventually settled into a peaceful existence ruled by a council of tribal chieftains. Defying expectations, they have advanced rapidly in natural magic and the arts, and their carvings, paintings and magic items are prized by collectors across the continent.

They face endless harassment from the orcs and other bestial tribes of the Feral Reach, who seem to want only to destroy what the nation has built. In recent years Ors has lost territory to raids, and the council is running out of ways to hold back the barbarians to the north.
