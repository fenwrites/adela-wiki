*A conglomerate of powerful city-states whose highest power is currency*


## Overview
**Peoples:** mixed, mostly humans, half-elves and halflings.

Less a nation than a conglomerate of mercantile city-states. The region was once embroiled in an endless territorial war backed by larger nations. Eventually those nations lost interest or were distracted by other problems, and the war finally ended. The uneasy peace that followed was slowly moulded by a series of trade and defence pacts between the now-distinct cities, which together form a powerful economic alliance, though the influence of their old patron nations still shows.

The cities are fiercely proud of their independence from the larger empires, giving their citizens greater freedoms and fewer laws. That also draws unsavoury types: cutthroat merchants, necromancers, slavers and other morally ambiguous trades can be found here, and gold rules above all else.
