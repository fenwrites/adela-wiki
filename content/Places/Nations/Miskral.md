## Overview
**Peoples:** mostly halflings.

What do you call a nation of "harmless" halflings living their best lives, sandwiched between two power-hungry nations? Miskral. Miskrallians are joyous, warm people who love ranching, a good meal and a warm bed. Perhaps they are just disarmingly charming; perhaps [[Hjirolis]] and [[Ors]] both prefer a buffer between them; perhaps their stranglehold on eastern ocean trade gives them a diplomatic edge. Whatever the reason, Miskral has survived some seven centuries without major conflict on its borders, even ceding small portions of land to Ors and to the indigenous bugbears of Svudek to keep the peace. But as tensions rise and expansionists grow more common, can the carefree nation survive?
