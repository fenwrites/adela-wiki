*Opportunistic order loving civilization of humans*

#nation


**Summary**
[[Hjirolis]], often shortened to Hjir is a human nation in the eastern half of [[Nosovae]]. It has an autocratic government, which is currently led by [[Autokrator Uluros IX]]. Hjir is built upon the ancient ruins of the Isiliosi people, with its capital city [[Ankarnassas]] being situated on the ancient glacier-fed Lake Atmos, where the largest still-standing remnants of Isiliosi architecture remains, including canals cut directly into the hard stone and the main government building. Apart from Ankarnassass, there are several notable agricultural cities in western and central Hjir, several notable mining, logging, and other industry in northern Hjir, and two military cities in the west along its borders with [[Ors]] and [[Miskral]].

**Geography and Populations**
[[Hjirolis]] is the third largest nation of [[Ucaro and Nosovae]], and the most populous of all nations on the continent. It's landscapes range from the limestone peaks of the Oronali Mountains to the north, rolling foothills, the massive [[Shimmerwyld]] on its western border, smaller forests, vast agricultural plains, ocean cliffs to the southwest, golden sand beaches in the central south, a mild desert called the Dalanos on its south-eastern border, and massive evergreen trees in rocky hills to the northeast. 

[[Hjirolis]] has a population of roughly 1 million people, of which 99% are humans. There are two main human races, the local and dominant Hjirrans, and the lower-class Sjuvven peoples. Hjirrans are relatively short, broad-shouldered, and olive-skinned, while Sjuvven are taller, paler, and lankier. The capital city [[Ankarnassas]] has a population of roughly 350,000, which is limited by its difficult mountains geography.

## Political Structure
The [[Autokrator Uluros IX|Autokrator]] is the supreme ruler. Beneath them:
- **Synedrion**: the high council of elder Archons, scholars, generals and temple leaders. It proposes laws, which the Autokrator ratifies.
- **Archons**: govern individual cities, with control of local trade, law, military and taxes.
- **Epistates**: regional overseers of villages, farms, temples and roads, often hereditary.
- **Dikanarchs**: roving imperial judges.
- **Grammateus**: scribes, record-keepers and tax collectors.
- **Polmarchs**: military governors, sworn to the Autokrator rather than the Archon.
- **Templarion**: heads of sanctioned temples.

In the countryside, an estate's **Oikonomos** is its ruling landlord and the town's political leader.

## Religion
The two patron Divines of Hjirolis are Order and History. Recently, the [[Divine of Order]] revealed itself and took the name Apolus.
