**Summary**
[[Hjirolis]], often shortened to Hjir is a human nation in the eastern half of [[Nosovae]]. It has an autocratic government, which is currently led by [[Autokrator Uluros IX]]. Hjir is built upon the ancient ruins of the Isiliosi people, with its capital city [[Ankarnassas]] being situated on the ancient glacier-fed lake Isin Io, where the largest still-standing remnants of Isiliosi architecture remains, including canals cut directly into the hard stone and the main government building. Apart from Ankarnassass, there are several notable agricultural cities in western and central Hjir, several notable mining, logging, and other industry in northern Hjir, and two military cities in the west along its borders with [[Ors]] and [[Miskral]].

**Geography and Populations**
[[Hjirolis]] is the third largest nation of [[Ucaro and Nosovae]], and the most populous of all nations on the continent. It's landscapes range from the limestone peaks of the Oronali Mountains to the north, rolling foothills, the massive [[Shimmerwyld]] on its western border, smaller forests, vast agricultural plains, ocean cliffs to the southwest, golden sand beaches in the central south, a mild desert called the Dalanos on its south-eastern border, and massive evergreen trees in rocky hills to the northeast. 

[[Hjirolis]] has a population of roughly 1 million people, of which 99% are humans. There are two main human races, the local and dominant Hjirrans, and the lower-class Sjuvven peoples. Hjirrans are relatively short, broad-shouldered, and olive-skinned, while Sjuvven are taller, paler, and lankier. The capital city [[Ankarnassas]] has a population of roughly 300 000, which is limited by its difficult mountains geography.
