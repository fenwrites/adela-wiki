## Overview
**Peoples:** mostly elves.

A quiet, secluded nation, bordered by the ocean on one side and [[The Seven Cities]] on the other. Xegora is a settler colony of elves washed far astray from distant lands. At first hostile to everyone, they slowly made peace and dug into their now-revered lands. They seem to have strange connections to the Fae who dwell there, and while they don't wholly ignore the Divines, they are perhaps the least religious people on the continent, apart from the occasional enclave of atheists.

Their love of seclusion and contemplative study of the natural world means little is known about Xegora's inner workings. But rumours that their motherland has contacted them, and of increased activity along their borders ([[Xegora's Contact from the Motherland]]), have put them on other nations' political radar.
