*The nation of the Naltése bugbears in southeastern Nosovae*

A nation of the [[Naltése Peoples|Naltése]] in southeastern [[Nosovae]], south of [[Miskral]]. Miskral has ceded small portions of land to its indigenous bugbears to keep the peace.
