## Overview
A multicultural and powerful empire, uniquely led by Empress Velesara, the [[Divine of Community]]. Though Velesara rarely seems involved in the mundanity of running an empire, she has reigned for three centuries and united several smaller nations. Being a good citizen (sitting on a town council, giving to charity) is the direct form of worship for Velesara, so there is an extremely strong sense of community and togetherness despite the empire's diversity, though that doesn't mean there are no divisions.

**Peoples:** mixed, mostly humans, elves, half-elves, halflings and dragonborn.

To spread this prosperity, worship of the [[Divine of Growth]] and the [[Divine of Currents]] has been welcomed, and diplomatic expansion has begun to the west, where Saers borders [[The Seven Cities]]. Expansion to the east has long been stalled by the [[Shimmerwyld]]. Recently a blight struck the crop fields near the forest ([[Green Death Blight]]), turning public sentiment against the enchanted woods. Rumours of something sinister awakening there are spreading, Velesara has not been seen for three years ([[Velesara's Disappearance]]), and for the first time in a long time, uncertainty and fear live in the empire.

## Geography
![[Saers Empire Map.jpg]]

Saers stretches from the Thrym Wastes and the Coldswood in the north to the southern coast, and from [[The Seven Cities]] in the west to the [[Shimmerwyld]] in the east. Its rivers include the Shardwater, the Vod'Akar and the Primon. Towns marked on the map include the capital [[Saers (City)|Saers]], Norbound, Greenhill, Orrensport, Malikan, Plainsview, Amalin, Balen's Crossing, Spokes, Pike, Labalhul, Rockwall, [[Wardensport]], [[Primon Boundary]] and [[Tollen Boundary]].
