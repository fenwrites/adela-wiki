*A sprawling farming town in Hjirolis near the Orreni lands*

A sprawling farming town in [[Hjirolis]] that has long outgrown its large limestone fort, which once stood guard against the denizens of the [[Shimmerwyld]]. [[Dotty Sunglade|Dotty]] and Sorven went there to treat with the Hjirolis people on behalf of the gnomes.
