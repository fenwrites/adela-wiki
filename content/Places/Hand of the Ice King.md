*A hostile and frozen northern region said to hold great artifacts*


## Overview
A hellscape of endless frozen hills and plains. Beneath the snow, it's said, lies an ancient civilization. Or an ancient monster. Or the body of a Divine. Nobody really knows, but a few adventurers have found ruins there holding old magic items. Reaching the remote region is not for the faint of heart.
