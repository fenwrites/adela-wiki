*A port-city of saers*

#town 
[[Saers]]

## Overview
A medium-sized port city that was once a free mercantile city before Saers absorbed it. It sits at the bottom of a valley on the far side of the Vod'Akar river, which empties into the Bay of Last Light. The Warden's Path bridge leads into town.

## Notable Features
- The Temple of Growth, built in an old decommissioned watchtower, with a stained-glass roof of a small tree amid battlefields. Head priest Orlas Vikkal, a gnome, blessed Delrek's axe.
- Bert's Books, run by Bert, a firbolg, where [[Klau]] found [[The Chronicle]], watched over by a brownie spirit named Stealy Git.
- The Drunken Drunkard tavern by the river, the Dawn Light Inn, Merric's Forge, and Enchanting Aromas (incense and magic items, run by Fiomilli, a halfling).
- The port, where the HES Longsail under Captain [[Azure “Hop”|Hop]] sailed for [[Hjirolis]].
