## Notable Features
- The Keep, an old, long-unused fort cleaned up to host the summit on the [[Shimmerwyld]], and to serve as a food bank during rationing.
- Across the river, the old ranger camp has grown into dozens of tents for researchers, scouts and soldiers.
- The Primon Boundary crest: a forking-river pendant made from sand from the riverbanks, enchanted by Naltese shamans for [[Sorhan Malikan]]'s ride into the Shimmerwyld. It protects the mind and acts as a compass.
