*A quiet and peaceful gnome grove*


## Overview
A quiet [[Inseore Gnomes|Inseore]] glade of about twenty families (36 gnomes), most living around the glade itself, with a creek running through it. Homes are modest wooden cabins. The one public building, the Hearthring, serves as meeting place, schoolhall, dining hall and courthouse, with a free-item table and a fire in winter.

## Notable Features
- Everyone gets a vote, even the children.
- School runs Monday to Thursday, and Sunday is market day. The glade runs mostly on trade.
- Arshanta is the only community deity, though personal beliefs are allowed.
