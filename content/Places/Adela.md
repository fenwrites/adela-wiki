*The entire world*


## Common Knowledge
- Adela orbits a single star, [[Lasaea]], and is circled by two small moons, [[Astera]] and [[Vora]]. For a few weeks each winter, the nearby blue star [[Shalar]] bathes the planet in a faint blue hue.
- Most people of [[Ucaro and Nosovae]] keep a calendar of ten months, divided by one-day periods called **Voids** ([[Void Night]]), when every star vanishes from the sky.
- The creators of the world, the Divines, are widely known to exist (see [[List of Divines]]).
- Technology is roughly High Middle Ages: steel is becoming common, empires are growing, wars are rarer but larger and more violent, and magic and science are advancing, though discoveries spread slowly. Gunpowder may exist, but would be extremely rare.
- Many regions have long histories, with ancient ruins and artefacts to match. Much of the world is settled, but large swathes remain hostile to people.
