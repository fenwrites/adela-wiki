*A town in Hjirolis, governed by Archon Leagro Manilis*

A town in [[Hjirolis]] governed by Archon Leagro Manilis.

## Activities and Rumours
- Four men named Ezolio live here: Ezolio Demallis, a miller; Ezolio Stamatilis, an accountant for the [[Antoniiadas Family|Antoniiadas]] farms; Ezolio Nassou, a volunteer for the [[Divine of Growth]]; and Ezolio Kappeas, a kindly-seeming old man who spent his days at the orphanage.
- Ezolio Kappeas was luring the town's children into the cult. The party killed him, then answered to the [[Fatebinders]].
