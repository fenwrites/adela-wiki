#town

## Overview
- **Location:** Northern [[Hjirolis]], at the foot of the [[Leukotichos]] Mountains
- **Est. population:** 350,000
- **Government:** Seat of [[Autokrator Uluros IX]]
- **Primary exports:** Stone, Magic, Religion
- The water in the lake is lower than it has been historically, and in fact slowly decreasing steadily
- There's a traditional marathon that runs around the entire lake. To celebrate [[Divine of Order|Apolus]] they are holding an extra-special one, about ten days after the party's arrival, to cap the forty days of celebration, with the winner receiving untold wealth and a direct, face-to-face meeting with the Divine. Entry is free, but only for noble families and those they sponsor. During the marathon is one of the few times that the grounds of the Order Church are open to the public.
- The official library of the [[Divine of History]] is in Ankar. Its chapels in the lower tiers each keep a small library, though there's none in Thema Pende.

## Thema Pende
*The newest tier, messy and under construction. The party is stuck here for now.*

Pende is overcrowded with pilgrims and travellers come to see the Divine. Near the gates to Tessra you'll find orderly lines, food vendors and markets, and much of the place has a party atmosphere, but tension runs high near its edges over the wall and the river.

It smells terrible, of manure and oily meat. People still talk about how muddy it was: the year looked set to be the rainiest on record, with floods in the Muds up in Thema Tessra, until Apolus appeared. The streets are rutted and dotted with muddy puddles. Buildings near the walls are stone; the further out you go, the more it's wood and ramshackle tents. Food and a bed run about 2 copper per person per day, and there isn't much in the way of high quality.

- The Wicks (wealthier folk sneer at these wooden homes as "wicks", since they'd burn), largely [[Sjuvven]]: a poor suburb of wood houses, tents and tradespeople, who serve the folk outside the wall or process raw goods coming into the city.
- Shade Valley: some distance up the road to the east. Even more disorderly, though more general chaos than real tension. The [[Astynomoi]] send out daily patrols to keep order.

### What's Here So Far
- **The permit queue,** by the gates to Thema Tessra. See [Getting Up a Tier](#getting-up-a-tier).
- **The Temple of [[Divine of Growth|Growth]],** an old stone-brick temple under [[Warden Einori]], caring for the poor through a spreading flu.
- **Food vendors and markets,** around the gates and the lines of pilgrims.
- **A mountainside stable** just out of town, run by Rhoda Kallis, where the party has camped in the pasture.
- **No library:** the chapels of the [[Divine of History]] keep small libraries in the lower tiers, but there's none in Pende.

## Getting Up a Tier
You can't walk up through the city without papers. Applicants queue near the gates to Thema Tessra, and the line barely moves through the crowds of pilgrims. Expect papers to take at least a week at a normal pace, unless you can find a quicker way.

## Rules of the City
- **Thema Pende and Shade Valley:** much less strict than the upper tiers, closer to anything-goes short of explicit crime. A new wall is being constructed to bring the area under control and to control movement through it.
- **Spells:** casting in public is illegal in the upper tiers.
- **Weapons:** carrying weapons openly is illegal in the upper tiers, apart from small weapons and staves in Tessra, Tria, Dio and Ena. All weapons are illegal in Ankar and Elikos, though a staff might pass as a walking stick.
- **Permits:** available for almost everything, including weapons. Annoyingly, each tier needs its own.
- **Guards:** see [[Astynomoi]].

## Who You'll Meet
Permanent residents are about 99% human, but this is a large capital that relies on imported food, so travelers are everywhere. A gnome is the rarest sight of all, though at a distance many people will take one for a halfling. Halflings are uncommon but not unheard of, since [[Miskral]] is a major trading hub nearby. The university is probably the most diverse place in the city, with maybe only 75% of its students human.

Bugbears are common enough, since the [[Naltése Peoples|Naltése]] live along the southern coast and a small village just southeast of the border sells fish into Hjirolis. Dragonborn are rarer, and are often seen as dignified beings, if more monstrous than humans. Reactions to foreigners are mild, especially in Thema Tessra and Thema Pende, though this can increase as you go up the tiers. You will stand out, however, so if you get a reputation, everyone will know who you are.

Humans are divided between two subraces, Hjirrans (~75% of population) and Sjuvven (~25%). See [[Hjir and Sjuvven Tensions]].

## Divine Orders
- **[[Divine of Order|Order]]:**
  - Ordinis Domos: the strict traditionalists, aligned with Apolus, with their seat in Elikos.
  - Ordinis Concordiae: mediators and lovers of debate, who see public consensus as order. Now in disarray.
  - Ordinis Cosmos: heavenly order, the order of the natural world.
- **[[Divine of History|History]]:** the official library in Ankar, with chapels and small libraries in the lower tiers.
  - Chronos Grammateus: the largest, concerned with history and law.
  - Chronos Paratiro: observation only.
  - Chronos Paradoseis: the library and the storykeepers.
  - Chronos Archaiologia: discovering knowledge.
  - Chronos Lyceum: spreading knowledge.
- **[[Divine of Growth|Growth]]:**
  - Auxesis Cyculus: agriculture.
  - Auxesis Fylakas: protecting and controlling nature. This is the chapter under [[Warden Einori]], at the old stone-brick temple in Thema Pende.
- **[[Divine of Community|Community]], or Civilization:**
  - Polis Synedrion: debates.
  - Polis Velesarae: favours the community model of [[Saers]].
- **[[Divine of Medicine|Medicine]]:**
  - Iasis Pharmakon: alchemy and preventative care.
  - Iasis Chirurgeon: major health crises.
  - Iasis Charatus: community health and outreach.
- **[[Divine of Freedom|Freedom]]:** Eleftheria, with a large [[Sjuvven]] following.
- **[[Divine of Warfare|War]]:** Polemos Dominus.
- **Craft:** chapels tied to the craft guilds.
- **Knowledge:** scholars and wizards around the Lyceum.
- **Gold:** officially looked down on, but worshipped in private by many merchants.
- **Death:** undertakers and gravekeepers.
- **Love:** chapels that handle weddings.
- **Luck:** small shrines everywhere, especially around the gambling tables.

## Districts
Ankarnassas is divided into seven holy districts. Elikos sits on the east side of Lake Atmos and Ankar lies along its shore; below them, five *themata* (cantons, written "Th.") step down the mountainside: Ena, Dio, Tria, Tessra and Pende. Thema Pende has its own section above. The Atmos River runs down through the lower cantons, and the North Pass leads out into the mountains.

### Thema Tessra
*Neatly planned, with the Muds at the bottom. The [[Kleptonomoi]], the city's lawful thieves, have a guild hall here.*
- Valleyhome: crowded with working-class families and tradespeople who are smart with their gold. Near the main road it's mostly businesses and taverns, the first proper shopping district. Newer, larger homes climb the mountain, for wealthier folk who don't mind a lower tier for lower costs.
- The Rises (Northrise and Southrise): home to wealthy merchants who care less about reputation, skilled tradesmen, and second cousins of noble families. Narrow roads and homes of two or three storeys, with an older first storey and newer ones above. It has three or four central markets, plus churches, schools and baths. The Kleptonomoi hall stands near the north wall.
- Low Pass: rising tradespeople and merchants who want newer homes. A bit scattered and only beginning to get organized, with markets huddled near the river and nicer homes going up the mountainside. Less respectable than the west side of the river, but there's nothing really wrong with it.
- Iasipi (the Muds): home to many of the city's [[Sjuvven]], along with some Hjirrans, poor working folk who were grandfathered into the wall. Active and full of manual labour, with children who run a bit wild, though even the young ones work. Old stone buildings, some collapsed and repaired with wood. It smells bad, except for the fresh river air near the water.
