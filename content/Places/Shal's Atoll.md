## Overview
A wandering island in the southwestern ocean, said to appear only to the most deserving sailors. It is said to be a paradise, with free food, drugs and entertainment for whoever stumbles upon it. Many a sailor laments in taverns that they've never seen it, and no sailor who claims to have found it has ever been fully believed.
