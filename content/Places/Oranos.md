## Overview
A smaller town in the foothills of the [[Leukotichos]], with a history of hosting nobility from the capital. It lies at the end of the Western Road, which follows the Agadion river past endless fields of wheat, cotton and goats, and the currant berries that are the region's main crop.

## Notable Features
- Sael Oros, "Lost Windsong": the lumpy, bare hills just outside town were part of the [[Shimmerwyld]] about a century ago, home to tall, reedy, hollow trees that sang in the cold mountain wind until they were cut down.
- Aromatica, a teahouse (in truth a brothel, since shut down) run by [[Gyrobastille]].
