*The ten months of the year, its festivals, and when each star sign is present*

Most people in [[Ucaro]] and [[Nosovae]] follow a calendar of ten months. Each month ends in a [[Void Night]], when all the stars vanish for a single night.

| Month | Days | Star signs and festivals |
|---|---|---|
| Morning Star | 30 | 1: Festival, the Year of the Dawnspirit. 4 to 11: the Choosing, in years it's held. 28: [[Star Signs\|the Soldier]] begins, and agricultural seeding begins |
| First Seed | 30 | 5: Festival, Early Market. 28: [[Star Signs\|the Empress]] begins, and Festival, Queen Maralow Day |
| Rain's Peak | 30 | 27: [[Star Signs\|the Beast]] begins |
| Lanathas | 30 | 23: [[Star Signs\|the Divine]] begins |
| Second Seed | 30 | 4: first harvest and second seeding begin. 19: [[Star Signs\|the Wayfarer]] begins |
| Sunbright | 30 | 20: [[Star Signs\|the Dragon]] begins |
| Hamnor | 30 | 6: Festival, the Elder's Circle. 20: [[Star Signs\|the Chameleon]] begins. 27: second harvest begins |
| Frostfall | 30 | 3: [[Star Signs\|the Mountain]] begins |
| Winterstar | 19 | 1: [[Star Signs\|Shalar]] begins |
| Diminus | 41 | 1: [[Star Signs\|the Faerie]] begins, and Festival, the Long Fire. 37: [[Star Signs\|the River]] begins. 40: Festival, Yearend |

The last day of every month is the Void.
