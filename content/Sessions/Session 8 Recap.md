*Harpies, an infernal monk, and the Divine of History*


## Recap
In [[Ceropolis]], the party saved a boy named Augies from harpies. Word of the rescue spread, and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] began to earn a reputation around the city.

[[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] was confronted by a shaven-headed monk working for infernal powers, who implied Tharr had been hiding from the consequences of his actions, and that those consequences were coming soon. The fight left Tharr unconscious.

[[Klau]] and Tharr also became involved with the [[Divine of History]].
