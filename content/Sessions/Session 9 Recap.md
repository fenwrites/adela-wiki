*Into the eastern Shimmerwyld; meeting Illesara*


## Recap
The party left [[Ceropolis]] and entered the [[Shimmerwyld]] from the [[Hjirolis]] side, where they met [[Illesara Glimmerglade]].

Deep in the forest they found a cave marked with the symbol of the [[Divine of Order]]. Inside was a planar portal to the infernal realm ([[Infernal In the Shimmerwyld]]).
