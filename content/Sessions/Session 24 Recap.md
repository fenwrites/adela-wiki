*A first night in Thema Pende*


## Recap
- [[Klau]] stayed at camp while the others went out. [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] shared tobacco with Pyles, an old man wearing a red patch, but learned nothing about it.
- At [[The Tall Order|the Tall Order]], [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]] and [[Twillius Unc|Twunk]] caught up. He told the party about local politics, [[Big Red]] and the [[Redwoods]].
- [[Mona]] talked with a drunk, melancholy member of the Ordinis Concordiae. She also confronted a former stable boy from her family's estate, who fled, and warned him to tell no one he had seen her.
- Delrek and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] stopped a grammateus leaving with a robed man. The robed man cut Tharr with a poisoned dagger before the clerk, Zenon, ended the fight and gave them a place to meet him at the wall.
- Delrek's hunter's mark showed the robed man heading up into the higher tiers before the spell ended.
- Delrek won over the dwarven masons, and he and Tharr went with them to a quarry across the Atmos bridge.
- On the way back they found Zenon dead. They told [[Bardas Korvas]] what they knew, with Tharr altering the fight slightly, and Korvas asked them to contact him the next day.
- At camp, [[Levi and Bram]] dispelled Klau's hut and tried to take [[The Chronicle]]. Klau dropped them with colour spray, healed Levi at Bram's plea, and kept their ring for now.
- Klau found the Chronicle had less magic, and that his writing no longer disappeared. A bad trip left it filled with repeated, unsettling lines.
- Hoping to reach the Saracens, Klau had Tharr knock him unconscious. Tharr took the Chronicle.
