*A first night in Thema Pende*


## Recap
With night falling over Thema Pende, [[Klau]] stayed behind to watch the camp in Rhoda Kallis's pasture, while the others went looking for a tavern. On the way they noticed a few locals wearing red patches on their arms. [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] sat down with an old man on a porch who wore one, a fellow who gave his name as Pyles, and the two shared some tobacco, but Pyles would say nothing about where the patch came from.

The party settled on the Tall Order, the tavern of [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illy]]'s old friend [[Twillius Unc|Twunk]]. Illy gave him a shortened account of everything that had happened to her, and the party asked him for news. The [[Green Death Blight|blight]] has not reached Pende, so he knew little of it, but he told them about local politics and all he knew of [[Big Red]] and the [[Redwoods]]. She and [[Mona]] stayed until closing so they could keep talking once the room had quietened down.

Mona, talking with the locals, recognized a man in the crowd as a former stable boy from her family's estate. She confronted him and tried to grab him, and he bolted from the building shouting for the guards. She let him go rather than make a scene, but not before warning him to tell no one where he had seen her.

[[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]], meanwhile, confronted a grammateus who was leaving with a robed man. Tharr claimed to be concerned for the clerk's safety, but the grammateus was going willingly. Blades came out briefly, and the robed man cut Tharr with a poisoned dagger before the grammateus called for everyone to stop. He gave them his name, Zenon, and told them where to find him at the wall for an appointment. The robed man taunted Tharr a little, and they went their separate ways, though not before Delrek placed a hunter's mark on him. Delrek felt the mark heading up into the higher tiers of the city, without being able to tell where, and then the spell ended.

Tharr joined the others in the bar, while Delrek fell in with the dwarven masons. Speaking Dwarvish won them over quickly, and they invited him to perform feats of drunken strength at a quarry outside town. Delrek and Tharr crossed the Atmos bridge in Pende, under the eyes of the guards there, who received one of Tharr's business cards, and walked another half hour to a stone-cutting camp on the west side. The dwarves are now friends of the party in town.

On their way back, Delrek and Tharr came across a small crowd huddled around a body. It was Zenon, the grammateus. They met [[Bardas Korvas]], a detective of the [[Astynomoi]], and made a good impression. They told him everything they knew of the clerk's movements, the robed man and the fight, with Tharr altering his part of that fight slightly. Korvas gave them his card and asked them to contact him the following day.

Back at camp, Klau had taken a mind-altering drug and settled in for the evening when two figures, [[Levi and Bram]], came creeping across the pasture, not nearly as quietly as they imagined. A ring of theirs dispelled his hut, but when they tried to take [[The Chronicle]] from his hands he cast colour spray, dropping Levi at once and very nearly Bram too. Levi was close to death. Bram begged Klau's forgiveness and asked him to heal his friend, and Klau did, keeping the ring for now. The pair told him where their camp lies outside town, and he may visit them.

Klau raised his hut again by ritual, and found that the Chronicle seemed to have less magic in it than before, and that what he wrote in it no longer disappeared. As the drug turned into a bad trip, he filled a stretch of it with repeated, unsettling lines.

The others returned to find him in the middle of it. Reasoning that he had only reached the Saracens when close to death, Klau convinced Tharr to knock him unconscious. Tharr obliged, and took the Chronicle for safekeeping as Klau slipped into the dark.
