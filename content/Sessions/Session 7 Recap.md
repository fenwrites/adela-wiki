*Across the sea to Ceropolis*

## Recap
The party sailed roughly 3,000 km aboard the HES Longsail, reflecting on the long voyage and growing into level 3. The passengers were memorable:
- Captain [[Azure “Hop”]], one-legged, possibly an ex-pirate, and well respected by her crew.
- [[Argus Cooky|Argus "Cooky"]], the ship's legendary orc cook, full of tales of the sea.
- [[Lady Aleera Valeteene]], a blue dragonborn merchant, travelling to a marriage that would join a powerful trading empire.
- [[Tyna Smallfoot]], a cheerful halfling messenger who invited the party to visit her home.
- [[Quintus Maximus]], a silent, watchful envoy from [[Hjirolis|Hjir]].

At the port of [[Pelethra]] they were met by [[Othenios Vyrik]], a local guide and historian, who led them into [[Ceropolis]]. They passed a marble gate honouring Apolus, the [[Divine of Order]]. In Tarmios Square they heard a passionate speech about reclaiming the northern lands. 

[[Fibbart Miller|Fibbart]] delivered a formal greeting to Archon [[Aoran Aurelius]], along with a gemstone tribute from [[Saers]] worth around 50,000 gold. The Archon accepted, coolly, and lodgings were arranged in the eastern wing. Just before the meeting, [[Mona]] unexpectedly dropped into the scene.

## Lore
- Ceropolis reveres the ancient Isiliosi and Apolus, the Divine of Order.
- The Chain of Accord, a binding pact between tribes, ultimately led to the Isiliosi's fall.
