*The road to Wardensport*

## Recap
The party left [[Primon Boundary]] for the port of [[Wardensport]], followed by [[Garnet Teimster]]. They ran into trouble from the [[Banditry in Saers|bandits]] that the blight's food shortages had driven onto the roads. 

In Wardensport, the party regrouped at a tavern while waiting for the ship to leave, where they met [[Fibbart Miller]], the envoy who would represent [[Saers]] on the mission, and [[Odd Job]], a blunt and capable mercenary. [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] noticed a sailor, Lucky Bron, bearing a strange mark above his head, and hired Odd Job to investigate.

While wasting time in town, Klau stumbled upon a small and shabby bookstore, 'Bert's Books', ran by a rare Firbolg. Inside what a strange and mischievous creature, and Klau stumbled upon a strange blank book: [[The Chronicle]].
