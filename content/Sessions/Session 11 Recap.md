*The blight reaches the Glimmerglade*

## Recap
The party discovered that the blight had reached the forest around the [[Glimmerglade]]. They warned the gnomes, who decided to move north to the [[Sunglade]] and regroup there. [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] and [[Mona]] had caught the blight, but both were cured by Delrek.

On the outskirts of [[Almurbalarammi]] the party saw smoke in the distance, and were questioned by a Couatl that transformed into an extremely tall, pale skinned, light-haired man. He demaded to know their purpose, and informed them that he had destroyed a sect of cultists. He decided the party was no threat, and swiftly left, mentioning he was searching for something for his master.
