*The eve of the Choosing, and its first exams*

# Recap
On the night before The Choosing, [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] undertook Naltechikakua, the [[Naltese Peoples|Naltese]] adulthood rite, overseen by the elderly chieftain [[Mahisakueu]]. In the meditative rite he chose the At'Aka (Tidewalk), and faced its trial (Gaining +1 Wis).

[[Klau]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] attended a tense family dinner. Klau's father, [[Sterling Starlight II]], handed him a commission for ceremonial robes, to be made alongside the warm and talented rival tailor [[Isadore]] before the final Choosing ceremony on the 11th.

The first day of The Choosing was a written and oral exam covering politics, magic, logic and religion. The second day offered a combat tournament.
