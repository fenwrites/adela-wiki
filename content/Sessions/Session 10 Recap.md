*The Inseore gnomes of the Glimmerglade*


## Recap
The party reached the [[Glimmerglade]], home of [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]'s people, the [[Inseore Gnomes]]. There they met the elder storyteller [[Florence Glimmerglade]], who welcomed them, worried about their warning.

The party spent the night with Illy's parents,  [[Thibido Stonyglade]] and foul-mouthed but fatherly inventor, and [[Loette Glimmerglade]], a patient and caring teacher.

[[Mona]] rejoined the group.

In the morning, the party investigated a minor rumor of a wild and vicious vorebeast that had been prowling outside the village, strange behaviour. They found it infected with the blight and killed it.
