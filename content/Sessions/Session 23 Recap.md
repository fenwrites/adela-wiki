*Arrival in Ankarnassas*


## Recap
The party woke on a hill overlooking the last few hours of road to [[Ankarnassas]]. They had spent three months travelling, fighting, and dealing with conflicts beyond anything they could have imagined, and everyone was slow to rise, whether from a hangover or the weight of the journey. After a pleasant morning in camp, they set off for the capital of [[Hjirolis]].

In town, they had planned to travel to the higher tiers and begin their work, but quickly realized without their diplomat status, they had no chance of being admitted through the throng of pilgrims that had come to town.

The party learned of a growing tension between Sjuvvens and Hjirolis peoples, and a rebel known as Big Red who was supposedly stoking the flames and fighting for equality for the Sjuvven. Delrek met with the local chapter of the Divine of Growth, who were dealing with their own problems and a spreading sickness (not green death), and asked him to venture to a nearby cavern to collect herbs for it, though he declined.

The party spent half the day in line to get papers to progress up a tier of the city, but line was hardly moving. 

With night falling, the party found a mountainside stable just out of town to house their horse, and convinced the proprietor, a redhead with an interest in dragonborn, to set up their tent in her pasture. With night setting, Klau cast Leomund's tiny hut and promised to watch their stuff, while the rest of the party went to a local tavern to do some sleuthing.
