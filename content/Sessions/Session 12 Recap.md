*The fight at Almurbalarammi*

## Recap
The party reached [[Almurbalarammi]], an ancient dome-shaped ruin two days northwest of the [[Glimmerglade]], built of a strange smooth metallic woody material. Gnome children grow up on ghost stories about the place, but it is famously boring: an entryway and a dusty chamber. A faded inscription on the wall reads: *"Almurbalarammi: A Fragile Creation, In One Day Its Name Breaks It."*

This time there were signs of a battle: an acid-burned door and dead cultists outside. A Couatl had raided the site before the party arrived, and had killed a devil. Its body bore a [[Zanangathor|Z symbol]], and inside its corpse was a smashed green gemstone. A few cultists remained in the inner chamber, and a frantic fight followed. [[Klau]] and [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] were each knocked unconscious twice, and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] once, but the party won through, reached level 4, and knocked out the cultists' leader to interrogate her.

They also recovered a pale green gemstone with some kind of being trapped inside. When [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]] touched it, a voice asked her: *"Help me."*
