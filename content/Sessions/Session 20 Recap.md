*Letters from home, and arrival in Oranos*

## Recap
The session opened around the fire, with everyone sharing a story from their lives.

The party arrived in [[Oranos]] in the evening: a small, hilly former logging town in the foothills of the [[Leukotichos]], on the fringe of the [[Shimmerwyld]].

At Aromatica, the party finally met with [[Gyrobastille]], a Saers informant they had been instructed to meet with. There, a bundle of letters caught up with the party:
- **From [[Garnet Teimster|Garnet]]**, in [[Ceropolis]]: she missed them terribly, was enjoying the palace's teas and baths, and was learning to be a merchant with [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]'s gold (with one loss, on a wine deal to [[Miskral]]). [[Fibbart Miller|Fibbart]] had become a friend. She'd heard Sjuvven labourers were leaving the docks, and that [[Lady Aleera Valeteene]]'s marriage into the [[Antoniiadas Family]] would expand their mining. She sent 30 gold and a patch for each of the [[Primon Boundary|Primon]] three: a three-claw signature for [[Klau]], a tree for [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]], and a scale for Tharr. Fibbart added that relations between [[Saers]] and [[Hjirolis]] were going well, and that he hoped to set up a proper embassy.
- **From Delrek's mother**, to Delrek and Klau: their little sister Samira was sick with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]], though fighting it with [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]]'s help. [[Primon Boundary]] had emptied out with only priests and soldiers remaining, and [[Tollen Boundary]] was deserted. Their families had been relocated to just outside [[Wardensport]].
- **From Justice [[Morro Shanderby]]** to Tharr, Klau and Delrek: *"You are relieved immediately of your duty to Saers."* They got fired for all their blunders.

The party spoke with Gyrobastille, who refused to give them information as they were no longer technically employed by Saers. She instead implied they would continue helping their families and nation, just not in an official capacity, and promised information if they party swore a simple oath and allowed a magical band to be placed on their skin with an enchantment that would inform Gyrobastille if they had been compromised. They all agreed (Illy, reluctantly). 

Gyro began to inform them of the chaos happening in Ankarnassas, and her own doubts about the validity of the new Divine, but was interrupted by guard captain that she had to dealt with. She gave the party rooms that had enchantments of silence protecting them from the outside, and left to deal with it.

In the night, Illy cut off her toe upon which the enchantment had been laid, severing it.
