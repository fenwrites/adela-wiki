*Back in Primon Boundary as adults; the summit and Pollard Farms*

## Recap
Years later, the group reunited in [[Primon Boundary]]. The [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] was worsening and food was running short. [[Waylan Wiles]] shared his theory that chaos magic in the [[Shimmerwyld]] was increasing from one direction, possibly from an old cultist fort.

A tense summit in the Keep debated whether to clear-cut the Shimmerwyld:
- [[Orlean Vetiver]] challenged Lord [[Audric Malikan]]'s authority and proposed that a War Mantle be given to someone else if Audric refused to act. [[Amansti Warbler]] backed him.
- [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]] stayed noncommittal. [[Mahisakueu]] urged caution.
- [[Siobhan Hearth]] gave grim projections for the food stores, and [[Glory Belle]] estimated four months to gather troops.
- Justice [[Morro Shanderby]] granted two weeks before a vote on expansion, or on a change of leadership.

[[Klau]], [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] and [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] all took active roles in the debate.

Waylan then asked the party to investigate Pollard Farms, tipped off by the travelling halfling bard Beatnick. On the way, they found a farmhouse that was being actively raided by bandits, who claimed that food shortages had meant they had to turn to banditry or starve, and carrying a note signed "AP". At the farm they fought Blighted zombies and found evidence of coercion, threats to abduct a child, forbidden seed storage and cult activity, along with silver coins laid around the doors and windows as a ward against demons.

Camping outside of Tollen Boundary, away from the Pollard Farm, the party was ambushed by several robed cultists. After defeating them, upon inspection the party found black steel spikes embedded at the base of their skulls.
