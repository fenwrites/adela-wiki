*Escorting the gnomes through the Shimmerwyld*

## Recap
Tensions ran high as the party escorted the gnomes. [[Mona]] questioned everyone about their pasts, and learned more about [[Saers]] and about the [[Dawn of the Midnight Sun]], who worship the distant blue star [[Shalar]], under the light of which Klau was born.

Nervous about [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]]'s growing fixation on the large green gem, Mona handed the gem over to him. When [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]] tried to stop her, [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] cast Command on her. Illesara fled, hid with Rope Trick and Invisibility, and tried (and failed) to steal the gem back. After talks with [[Loette Glimmerglade|Loette]] and [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]], Tharr gave the gem to Delrek, who handed it back to Illesara.

That night, Tharr's infernal dreams tormented him. His request for a review of his contract was approved, and for the first time he spoke with [[Murhz’egellaghorighhtz|Murhz]] and his imp assistant, [[Bip|Bipiligus]], who struck Tharr with a psychic attack on command. [[Klau]] soothed Tharr in his sleep, and Tharr talked Bip into a quiet understanding: *"You scratch my back and I'll scratch yours."* Bip promised to visit again the next night.

The gnomes set off east to skirt the edge of the [[Shimmerwyld]], with [[Dotty Sunglade|Dotty]] and Sorven travelling with them. Klau spent much of a night helping Henrietta Sunglade tailor robes, and Sorven took to working with the horses.

Along the way, the caravan met Anax [[Cyphus Orreni]], a young noble of [[Hjirolis]], and his bugbear guard, [[Graveltooth]]. Cyphus was searching for his nephew Iocles, missing in the Shimmerwyld for two weeks, and the two joined the caravan when some of the gnomes thought they had seen the boy to the north. On the road, the party cured the blight from a root shrine to Arshanta, where Delrek saw a vision of cultists closing in on the [[Sunglade]]. They also caught a cultist hiding with a bow and wyvern poison, and fought a blighted owlbear that knocked Delrek unconscious. On a Void Night, the green gemstone glowed and spirits gathered around it. With the gnomes nearing the edge of the forest, the party set out to search Iocles's last known location, a witch hut in the Witchwood, but found no sign of him.

The party defeated a shambling mound and found a secret entrance in the witchwood, but left it to answer a danger signal. A massive earthquake struck the landscape, and Delrek tapped into Shimmer, partially transforming in front of his friends, to decrease the damage to the gnome caravan. The session ended with the party on the trail of [[Tonks and Tansi]] and three children, who had gone missing.
