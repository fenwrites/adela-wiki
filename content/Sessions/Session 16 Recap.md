*Leaving the gnomes and learning of Order*


## Recap



The party escaped to the edge of the Shimmerwyld after rescuing [[Tonks and Tansi|Tonks, Tansi]], and their children from another ruin like Almurbalurammi. In that place, they found a map depicting several ruins both within the current extent of the Shimmerwyld, and outside of it.

On the edge of the Shimmerwyld, a feast was held, and the gnomes began to set up a temporary camp. Dotty and Sorven planned to head to [[Topetki]] to politic with the Hjirolis peoples, and the party agreed to join them, as they had business up the road in [[Oranos]].

Cyphus Orreni agreed to escort the party to his families estate. Along the way, they entered his families farmland, and from a farmhand Cyphus learned and shared with the party that in the past few weeks, it had been announced that [[Divine of Order|Apolus]], Divine of Order, had come to lead Hjirolis into greatness.
