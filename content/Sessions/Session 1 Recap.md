*The Choosing comes to Primon Boundary*

## Recap
The story began in [[Primon Boundary]], a frontier town on the eastern edge of the [[Saers]] Empire, ruled by the Divine-Ruler Velesara ([[Divine of Community]]). The town was founded by [[Sorhan Malikan]], who long ago drove a cult out of the [[Shimmerwyld]].

The town was preparing for The Choosing, a festival marking its children's passage into adulthood. Proctor [[Melivio Korsostai]] arrived from Saers with his assistants [[Amansti Warbler]] and [[Grobak Skullsmasher]] to oversee the examinations.
