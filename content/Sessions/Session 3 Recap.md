*Last preparations before the Shimmerwyld*

## Recap
Before heading into the [[Shimmerwyld]], [[Finch]] said goodbye to his worried mother, Jean. At his home the group found a small book of folktales, *The Saers' Call: Legends of the Old Stars*.

At the Temple of Growth, the dwarf priest [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]], one of the few dwarves living in [[Saers]], gave blunt advice for the forest: ignore every distraction. No voices, no food, no reading. His temple's stained glass shows a violent scene from history, a reminder that destruction and growth go hand in hand.

Across the river, the rangers [[Ayauheu]] and [[Erasmus]] warned of whispers in the woods and of trade caravans that had vanished. Ayauheu recognised that [[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] had completed his rite. With the rangers' directions in hand, the group set out for the cave entrance at the edge of the Shimmerwyld.

The party, Finch, and Garnet stumbled into a cave, where they found a signs of someone living there. Tharr read a note in infernal left on a table, a miraculously resisted its malicious influence, though a mark now hung over his head. At that moment a devil returned, and a battle ensued. The kids were quickly overwhelmed, with garnet having her leg snapped in two when Delrek tried to pull her away from the devil.

At the last moment, they were saved by [[Grobak Skullsmasher]].

Brought to safety, they were questioned, but the incident was quickly moved past. Before they knew it, they had been offered a place in [[Brussaer]], the famed college of [[Saers (City)]]. Garnet was unable to go, still recovering from her leg injury, and Delrek decided to stay behind and apprentice with [[Adoniram Gromsh]] as an acolyte to the [[Divine of Growth]].

Klau and Tharr went to the capitol city.

## People Met

- [[Adoniram Gromsh|Gromsh]], priest of the [[Divine of Growth]]
- [[Ayauheu]], a calm and cautious bugbear ranger
- [[Erasmus]], a talkative human ranger
- Barlowe, a half-orc butcher
