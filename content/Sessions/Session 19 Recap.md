*Ezolio's deceit, the Fatebinder, and travel*


The party got Ezolio, who had been running an orphanage and abducting orphans to turn into cultists, but had to explain to the [[Fatebinders]], a faction of law-enforcement and judges in Hjirolis, what had happened. They were released, but it was implied that these actions greatly harmed their status as diplomats from Saers.

In Ezolio's cellar, the party found ritual items and notes on the orphans he had been sending to the cult. The Fatebinder who released them warned them not to abuse their diplomatic power, but it was already too late: word of their conduct cost them their posts as Saers' representatives.
