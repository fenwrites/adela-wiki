*Nettle, the evacuation, and a voice in the Chronicle*

## Recap
In the [[Sunglade]], [[Thibido Stonyglade|Thibido]] shared an idea with [[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]]: [[Vivica Glimmerglade|Vivica]] once used a rare clear flower that filters out magical toxins to clean up one of his accidents. Might it help against the blight? Vivica, discussing bee transport with the beekeeper Agnes Sunglade, explained that these Glasspetal flowers grow in swampy parts of the [[Shimmerwyld]], feeding on toxins rather than sunlight. [[Dotty Sunglade|Dotty]] knew of a swamp nicknamed the Witchgrove, northeast of the Sunglade, with an old wooden house inside.

At the next day's meeting, [[Agatha Sunglade|Agatha]] ordered the [[Sunglade]] and [[Glimmerglade]] to evacuate north to the caverns of [[Stonyglade]]. Rangers were sent out: [[Wolby Glimmerglade|Wolby]] to warn the Meadowglade and the Alluara gnomes, Siella to check on the Mossyglade, and Zazzo ahead to Stonyglade.

Wolby returned with a tattooed gnome claiming to be from the Meadowglade, who said the village was destroyed. When Dotty drew her quarterstaff, the stranger laughed it off and transformed: he was [[Nettalytyn Nyx]], "Nettle". He warned that cultists had destroyed Mossyglade,  took a keen interest in the party: in [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]], who holds both Divine and spirit power; in [[Mona]], potentially possessing witch-like powers; and in Illesara, a local with strangely no spirit about her. He invited them to his camp north of the glade for tea.

The party chose to escort the gnomes to the edge of the Shimmerwyld rather than head to the World Tree or [[Oranos]]. Nettle heard about the Couatl, the [[The Chronicle|Chronicle]] and Delrek's life story. He told them of the opposition between the [[List of Divines|Divines]] and the [[Spirits]], and suggested that if they wanted to save their families, they should stop the Divines from entering the Shimmerwyld.

At some point, words appeared in [[The Chronicle]]: *"Hello. My name is Elowen Saracen. Sorry if I scared you."* They erased themselves as [[Klau]] watched. Klau wrote back, "Are you the woman of my dreams?" The reply: *"Umm, I don't think so. I'm just a girl. And you were dreaming? I haven't slept in so long that I almost forget what that's like."*
