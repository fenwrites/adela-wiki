*The harpy, Aromatica, and the road to Ankarnassas*


## Recap
The party defeated a coven of harpies. Their massive leader had ragged wings and a necklace of small animal bones, and was performing a ritual of spirits and sacrifice inside a circle of runes from the Sinok, a druidic, chaotic people from north of the [[Leukotichos]]. She had sensed a surge of power from [[The Chronicle]] and bewitched [[Klau]] into bringing her the book.

While bewitched, Klau found himself in an ethereal place, where he met the Saracen family. They told him they had lived there for thirty years, while three centuries passed outside, and gave a cryptic warning: entities of great destruction, the [[List of Divines|Divines]], are sealed away in the stars themselves. The seal is weakening, and they are fated to return within seven years.

When the harpy fell, she dropped a black spiked censer that now faintly burns with green fire and smoke in [[Mona]]'s hands. The building turned out to be an old bastion, [[Bastion XIII]] by the plaque in its rubble, a structure supposedly built by the ancient Isiliosi, though the party doubted that. Beneath the crumbling fort was a dungeon entrance that someone had deliberately hidden, despite public archaeological efforts. The party took a few steps inside, retreated, and hid the entrance again.

Back in Aromatica, [[Gyrobastille]] was leaving for [[Miskral]], since a new law had outlawed her bordello. She warned that [[Hjirolis]] was changing, though things seemed to be calming: travel was fine with the right permits ([[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] spent half a day getting a horse permit for Vauve). She warned of possible signs of an Edict, a powerful form of Divine magic that can alter whole regions, rarely for the better. Though Klau, [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] and Tharr had essentially been fired as [[Saers]]' representatives, she suggested they could still help with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] by learning more about it. The signs she'd heard of were strange weather and odd bird migrations. Bureaucracy was out in full force and taxes were rising, but she had pulled strings to get the party a traveller's permit so they wouldn't be arrested on the highway, and she left her assistant Denoument in [[Oranos]] to keep gathering information.

In return, the party told Gyro what they knew: infernal rituals and portals, runes of Order, maps to Isiliosi ruins, the gnomes' political situation, infernal lords trying to get into the [[Shimmerwyld]], the seven-year warning, [[The Chronicle]], and Bert's Books.

The party set off for [[Ankarnassas]]. On their last night on the road they danced and listened to music with a travelling band of worshippers of the [[Divine of Rhythm]]. Illesara succumbed to the song and felt a whisper of the Divine connection that true worshippers feel.
