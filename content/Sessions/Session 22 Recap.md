*The harpy, Aromatica, and the road to Ankarnassas*


## Recap
The party defeated a coven of harpies. Their massive leader had ragged wings and a necklace of small animal bones, and was performing a ritual of spirits and sacrifice inside a circle of runes from the Sinok, a druidic, chaotic people from north of the [[Leukotichos]]. She had sensed a surge of power from [[The Chronicle]] and bewitched [[Klau]] into bringing her the book.

While bewitched, Klau found himself in an ethereal place, where he met the Saracen family. They gave a cryptic warning: entities of great destruction, the [[List of Divines|Divines]], are sealed away in the stars themselves. The seal is weakening, and they are fated to return within seven years.

When the harpy fell, she dropped a black spiked censer that now faintly burns with green fire and smoke in [[Mona]]'s hands. The building turned out to be an old Bastion, a structure supposedly built by the ancient Isiliosi, though the party doubted that. Beneath the crumbling fort was a dungeon entrance that someone had deliberately hidden, despite public archaeological efforts. The party took a few steps inside, retreated, and hid the entrance again.

Back in Aromatica, [[Gyrobastille]] was leaving for [[Miskral]], since a new law had outlawed her bordello. She warned that [[Hjirolis]] was changing, though things seemed to be calming: travel was fine with the right permits ([[Tharr Brirr|Tharr]] spent half a day getting a horse permit for Vauve). She warned of possible signs of an Edict, a powerful form of Divine magic that can alter whole regions, rarely for the better. Though Klau, [[Delrek Hammersmak|Delrek]] and Tharr had essentially been fired as [[Saers]]' representatives, she suggested they could still help with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]] by learning more about it.

The party set off for [[Ankarnassas]]. On their last night on the road they danced and listened to music with a travelling band of worshippers of the Divine of Rhythm. Illesara succumbed to the song and felt a whisper of the Divine connection that true worshippers feel.
