*Klau's nightmare, and the walk to the tower*

Klau had terrible dreams, a nightmare, and was taken possession of by an unknown force. In the morning, he began to walk. The party tried to stop him, but he seemed to slowly die when he was impeded, so they instead followed him out of town to a tower.
