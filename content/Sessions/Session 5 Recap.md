*Spore zombies, a dying boy, and a mission to Hjirolis*

## Recap
The investigation at Pollard Farms continued. The party fought zombies riddled with the [[Green Death Blight|Green Death]], which burst into clouds of spores as they died, risking infection for anyone nearby.

That night, a man named [[Ralof and Bjor|Ralof]] stumbled into camp, a refugee from [[Hjirolis]], begging for help for his son, Bjor. The party found the boy in his final throes from an untreated infection, one that amputation might have halted, or high-level divine magic cured.

Seeking guidance, Delrek entered the Shimmerwyld, and was approached by a strange creature of twisting vines, identifying themselves as [[Shimmer]], who promised power to cure the boy in exchange for access to Delrek's very being. Delrek agreed, and cured Bjor of his ailment.

Closer to Primon Boundary, the party stumbled upon [[Erasmus]]'s corpse, which animated and attacked them. They laid him to a final rest, then returned to Primon boundary and reported on their findings.

From there, the party was given three choices: Present their findings to the council in [[Saers (City)]], teturn to the shimmerwyld with [[Waylan Wiles]] and investigate the cultist fortress known to be to the southeast, or travel to Hjirolis and investigate the Shimmerwyld from its far side.

Head researcher [[Augustus Fearly]] urged them to take their findings to Saers, but they accepted Justice [[Morro Shanderby]]'s request instead: to travel to Hjirolis by way of [[Wardensport]], a journey of about seven weeks.
