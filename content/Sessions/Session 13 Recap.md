*Rami's escape, and the story of the Shimmerwyld*

## Recap
The cultists' leader introduced herself as [[Rami Ostensa]], a poor, confused girl from [[Oranos]] in [[Hjirolis]]. She mentioned [[Zanangathor]] in reference to the Z on the devil's body. The party didn't buy her story and left her tied up, but she slipped free and ran back to the ruin with a head start. [[Klau]] cast Knock to open a door that seemed nearly impossible to open otherwise, and dispatched her with a frost bolt.

With the cultists cleared out but few answers, the party returned to the [[Sunglade]], meeting a pair of quarrelling ents on the way. The [[Glimmerglade]] gnomes were settling in there, and the party met again with [[Florence Glimmerglade|Florence]] and with [[Agatha Sunglade]], leader of the Sunglade, and her assistant [[Dotty Sunglade]].

## The Story of the Shimmerwyld
Florence finally told the story of the [[Shimmerwyld]]'s origin:
- Before any nation, [[Adela]] was a sterile desert of magma fields, warmed by day by the star [[Lasaea]], with no stars at night.
- [[Arshanta (Being)|Arshanta]] watched, frustrated, until a piece of her crashed into Adela in a flash of white light. From it sprouted the World Tree. The gnomes call the forest *Dae Taure Inse*, "Grove of the Fallen Star".
- Much later, a people called the Isiliosi lived in harmony with the Shimmerwyld and built ruins like [[Almurbalarammi]].
- Gnomes following the [[Divine of Order]] arrived and tried to tame the wilds. The Isiliosi were nearly wiped out and vanished into the forest. Then the woods themselves came alive and nearly destroyed the gnomes.
- The gnomes split. The [[Alluara]] left for the southern coast to keep their inventions, and now live in caves under the water. The rest kept the name [[Inseore Gnomes|Inseore]] as a reminder of their shame, and live in harmony with the woods.

[[Illesara Glimmerglade|Illesara]] mentioned she had recently travelled to the World Tree, where the animals had become unusually aggressive.

The party showed Florence and Agatha the pale green gemstone holding a being of pure energy. Neither knew what it was, but Agatha suggested seeking counsel in [[Ankarnassas]].
